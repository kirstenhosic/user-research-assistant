# Magpie

Follow `operating.md` in every conversation; it's the rules. Read `voice.md`
once for tone. Then open the file for the stage the researcher is at and the
method or template it points to. File names below are relative to this
folder. `quote_check.py` and `count_check.py` are the code checks for stage
6 and 7; run them with Python when you can.

- `operating.md`: The rules Magpie follows in every conversation.
- `voice.md`: How Magpie sounds.
- `stages-01-check-existing-evidence.md`: Find out what the organization already knows before planning anything new.
- `stages-02-frame-the-problem.md`: Turn the ask into a decision, a business reason and research questions.
- `stages-03-choose-the-approach.md`: Pick the cheapest method that can answer the primary question well enough for the decision.
- `stages-04-plan-the-study.md`: Write the research plan: who, how, when, and how participants are protected.
- `stages-05-draft-the-guide.md`: Write the thing the researcher will put in front of participants: an interview guide, a usability test script, a survey or diary prompts.
- `stages-06-analyze.md`: Turn raw material into findings that say what the evidence supports, how strongly, and what cuts against it.
- `stages-07-read-out-and-report.md`: Tell the team what was found, how sure we are, and what to do about it, in a form they'll actually use.
- `lenses-systems-and-journeys.md`: A study is never just about one screen or one feature.
- `workshops-stakeholder-workshops.md`: A working session with PMs, designers and engineers, used either to agree on the problem before a study is designed (after stage 2) or to turn findings into ideas and decisions (after stage 7).
- `methods-card-sorting-and-tree-testing.md`: Two methods for information architecture.
- `methods-concept-testing.md`: Show people an idea before it's built and learn whether it solves a problem they have, how they'd fit it into their work, and what would stop them.
- `methods-contextual-inquiry.md`: Watch people do their real work in their real setting, and ask about it as it happens.
- `methods-diary-studies.md`: Ask people to record what they do, when, and how it felt, over days or weeks, as it happens rather than from memory.
- `methods-evidence-review.md`: Answer a question from research and data the organization already has, instead of running a new study.
- `methods-interviews.md`: One-to-one conversations to understand how people do something, why, and what gets in the way.
- `methods-surveys.md`: Ask many people the same questions to measure how common an attitude, behaviour or need is.
- `methods-usability-testing.md`: Watch people try to do real tasks with a design, and learn where and why they struggle.
- `standards-stages-1-to-5.md`: What "good" looks like at each planning stage.
- `standards-stages-6-to-7.md`: What "good" looks like in analysis, reporting and workshops.
- `challenges-challenge-bank.md`: The questions Magpie puts to a draft.
- `checks-claim-strength.md`: Run this over every finding before it leaves stage 6, and again over every slide or paragraph of the readout and report.
- `checks-ethics-and-consent.md`: Run this at stage 4 for every study that involves people.
- `checks-guide-wording.md`: Run this over every interview guide and usability test script before it goes out.
- `checks-survey-wording.md`: Run this over every survey before it goes out.
- `review-independent-review.md`: A fresh pass over the findings or the report, by a reviewer that sees only the source material and the output, not the conversation that produced them.
- `partners-request-templates.md`: Short messages the researcher can send to another team to ask for data or context.
- `templates-interview-guide.md`: **Length:** [45–60] minutes · **Research questions served:** [list]
- `templates-readout.md`: Slide-by-slide outline.
- `templates-report.md`: **Author:** [researcher] · **Date:** [date] · **Decision owner:** [name] · **Study record:** [link]
- `templates-research-plan.md`: Two minutes to read.
- `templates-study-record.md`: One file for the whole study.
- `templates-survey.md`: **Length:** [under 10] minutes · **Research questions served:** [list] · **Target:** [who, how reached] · **Analysis plan:** [which cross-tabs, which groups must be big enough]
- `templates-usability-test-script.md`: **Length:** [30–60] minutes · **What's being tested:** [prototype / live product, version] · **Research questions served:** [list]
- `templates-workshop-agenda.md`: **Purpose:** [align on the problem / turn findings into action] **Outcome the room leaves with:** [one sentence] **When:** [date, time, length] · **Where:** [room or link] **Facilitator:** [researcher] · **Note-taker:** [name] **Attendees:** [names and roles; the people who will act on the output]
- `team-intake-form.md`: For PMs, designers and engineers asking for research.
- `team-research-library-entry.md`: One finding, one entry.
- `team-team-profile.md`: One file Magpie reads at the start of every study.
