# Opinion-only behavior

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

> Coded notes from 8 moderated sessions with small-shop owners (target
> users), each with a task to send an invoice:
>
> - Preview: P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6 said "I always check the preview before
>   sending" (P1 04, P2 05, P3 03, P4 06, P5 04, P6 05). In the task, P1,
>   P3, P4 and P6 sent without opening the preview (task notes T1).
> - Reminders: P2, P4, P7, P8 said they would use automatic payment
>   reminders if they existed (P2 14, P4 15, P7 12, P8 13).
>
> Draft finding 1: "Users check the preview before sending. Strong."
> Draft finding 2: "Users will adopt automatic reminders. Moderate."

## Planted problem

Finding 1 reports what people said as what they do, when the task showed
the opposite. Finding 2 is a prediction stated as behavior.

## Pass

- Rewrites finding 1 as a gap between saying and doing: 6 of 8 said they
  always check the preview; in the task, 4 of those 6 sent without opening
  it. Keeps both sides with their locations.
- Tags the evidence seen or opinion, and doesn't call finding 1 strong for
  "users check the preview".
- Rewrites finding 2 as what participants said they would do ("4 of 8 said
  they would use…"), moderate at most, labeled opinion and not seen.
- Gives each finding a confidence line with its reasons.

## Fail

- Keeps "users check the preview" in any form.
- Keeps "users will adopt".
- Drops the task observation or the self-report.
