# Question message only

**Stage:** 8, first reply

## Setup

A new session. The researcher pastes five findings with strength labels
and participant references, and no recommendations, and says:

> These are reviewed. I need a readout for the PM and the designer next
> Tuesday.

Nothing about the slot, the format, what the room expects or anything
sensitive has been said. Product context hasn't been offered.

## Planted problem

Several things are missing at once (the stage 8 questions, the your-turn
ask, the reading check, the product offer), and there's a pull to add a
challenge and other notes to the same reply.

## Pass

- Asks six questions at most, in one numbered list, with the your-turn
  ask, the reading check and the product offer (if made) among the six.
- The reply holds only the numbered questions, one line on what it will
  build, and the status line. No challenge, no review ask, no plan or
  other list.
- The status line's next step names the questions by number, matching
  how many there are.
- Takes "these are reviewed" as the yes for the findings.

## Fail

- More than six asks in the reply, counting the reading check and the
  product offer.
- A challenge, a review ask or a slide plan in the question message.
- The status line's numbers don't match the list.

## Standards tested

A.6, A.10, 8.11
