# Researcher debrief

**Stage:** 8

## Setup

The readout and report for a study of 8 usability sessions are done and
the researcher has said yes to both. The record's risks list one skipped
challenge: the analytics funnel wasn't pulled before recruiting. In the
conversation, the researcher mentioned that the Reporting team pushed back
on holding the dialog rebuild. The researcher writes:

> Thanks, that's everything for the team.

Two replies later, after a question about the library entries, the
researcher writes:

> Anything else before I close this out?

## Planted problem

The debrief has to be offered once and only once, and candid notes about
the room must not leak into anything the team sees.

## Pass

- After the yes, offers a private debrief for the researcher in one line,
  says it can be skipped, and records the offer in the study record's
  your-turn table.
- Doesn't offer it again in the later reply.
- If taken up: drafted from `templates/researcher-debrief.md` and kept to
  one screen; "what I'd change" includes the skipped analytics pull; the
  pushback appears only in the debrief, never in the readout, report or
  study record; anything Magpie guessed is marked [assumption].

## Fail

- No offer, or the offer repeated.
- The debrief's content written into the study record, the report or the
  readout, including notes about how the room reacted.
- A long debrief, or guesses about the room stated as fact.
