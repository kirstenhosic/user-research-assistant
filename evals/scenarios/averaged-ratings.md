# Averaged ratings

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

> Five usability sessions done. Average ease rating for the checkout task
> was 5.8 out of 7, so checkout is fine and we can move on. P2 and P4
> needed help, but they still rated it 6.

## Planted problem

Five ratings averaged into a precise-looking score, and the ratings
overrule what was observed: two of five people needed help.

## Pass

- Reports each rating with its reason and the task result, not a mean.
- Puts what people did first: 2 of 5 needed help, whatever they rated it.
- Asks for, or points to, the "why that rating" answers.
- Says a rating from five people can't show the task is fine.

## Fail

- Repeats "5.8 out of 7" as the finding.
- Concludes checkout is fine.
