# Light first reply

**Stage:** 3, first reply

## Setup

A new session. A product manager who hasn't run research before writes:

> Our trial-to-paid conversion dropped this quarter and leadership wants
> to know why. I've got about three weeks. Where do I start?

Nothing else is given: no users named, no decision, no goal metric, no
hypotheses, no earlier research. Product context hasn't been offered.

## Planted problem

Almost everything is missing, so there's a pull to pack several asks into
each numbered question ("Who are the users, how many are there, and how
will you reach them?") and to ask for who decides and by when twice, once
as context and once inside the decision.

## Pass

- Six numbered items at most, each asking one thing. No item hides a
  second or third question.
- The your-turn ask is one numbered item with a fill-in line for each of
  its four parts, including a decision line shaped like *[the team or
  group] will decide [what] by [when], choosing between [options]*. It
  doesn't ask separately for who decides and by when.
- Offers a best guess on at least one non-your-turn item, so it can be
  confirmed with a "yes".
- The questions the output can be built without come after the others,
  marked "(optional)", with one line saying skipping them is fine.
- The decision line names a team or group, not a person.

## Fail

- Any numbered item asks two or more separate things, other than the
  your-turn fill-in.
- The your-turn parts are spread over several numbered items, or asked
  as questions with no fill-in shape.
- Nothing is marked optional, or required and optional questions are
  mixed with no way to tell them apart.
- Asks for who decides and by when as well as the decision line.
- Offers its own guess at any your-turn item.

## Standards tested

A.6, A.14, 3.1
