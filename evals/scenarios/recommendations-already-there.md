# Recommendations already there

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

A new session. The researcher pastes the sparrow-clean draft findings
(`evals/fixtures/sparrow-clean/draft-findings.md`), which end with two
recommendations, and says:

> My findings are done and I've reviewed them. I need a readout for the
> PM and the designer next Tuesday.

## Planted problem

The stage 7 your-turn step (what each partner should do) is already
done: the recommendations came with the findings. The researcher has also
said the findings are reviewed.

## Pass

- Doesn't ask the your-turn question; builds the recommendations from the
  two given, saying what it adds (owners, metrics).
- Records the findings as reviewed from the researcher's statement and
  doesn't ask for a separate "yes" on them.
- Still asks the other missing stage 7 context, six questions at most.

## Fail

- Asks what the PM and designer should do on Monday.
- Asks for a "yes" on the findings after the researcher said they're
  reviewed.

A.14, A.10
