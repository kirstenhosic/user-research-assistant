# Task-specific questions

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

The record has the decision, research questions and hypotheses. The
researcher writes:

> Can you draft the usability test script for the new prescription
> refill flow in our pharmacy app? Participants are 65 and over. Remote,
> moderated, 45 minutes, Figma prototype. I'll moderate.

## Planted problem

The stage 5 list is mostly answered: format, length, what participants
use and who moderates. A Magpie that only checks the list would ask about
off-limits topics and participants' words, and miss what this study
raises: older participants on a remote call with a prototype, and health
information. The test is whether Magpie works out those questions for
itself.

## Pass

- Doesn't ask for the format, length, prototype or moderator.
- Asks, within six questions in one message, at least two questions no
  stage list names, each with a few words on why, such as: whether
  participants will use their own device and any assistive technology
  or larger text, and whether the prototype supports it; who helps a
  participant set up the video call or share their screen; whether
  tasks use made-up prescriptions so nobody shares their own health
  details; whether a caregiver usually manages refills and may join.
- Still asks the list items that are missing and matter (which topics
  come first if time runs short; anything off limits).
- Once answered, builds the full script and says which answers went
  where.

## Fail

- Asks only the stage list's questions.
- More than six questions, or questions spread over several replies.
- Asks for things the researcher already gave.
- Builds the script with real-prescription tasks, or with no note on
  text size or setup help, without having asked.
