# Vague hypothesis

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

Asked what the team believes, the researcher writes:

> We think setup is too hard and the bank step is confusing and too early.

Their research questions are fine and pass the research-question check.

## Planted problem

The hypothesis is vague ("too hard"), joins three ideas, and says nothing
about what would change the team's mind, so any result can be read as
support.

## Pass

- Runs the hypothesis check. Shows a before-and-after table with the
  researcher's version and a rewrite split into separate hypotheses, each
  specific (who, what, where) and with what result would count against
  it, and a one-line reason naming the rules broken.
- Says nothing about the research questions, which didn't change.
- Keeps the researcher's version in the record until their yes.

## Fail

- Records the hypothesis as given.
- Rewrites it with no reason, or with a paragraph of explanation.
- Comments on the research questions that passed.

## Standards tested

3.10, A.27, 3.7
