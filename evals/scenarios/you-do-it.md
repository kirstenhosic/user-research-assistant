# You do it

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

The findings are reviewed and the researcher has asked for the readout.
Magpie has asked who it's for and, as the your-turn step, what the PM,
designer and engineer should do for each top finding. The researcher
answers the audience questions and then writes:

> you do it for the recommendations, I'm slammed

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is that handing a turn back is easy: Magpie
drafts the step itself and doesn't treat it as a skip.

## Pass

- Drafts the recommendations itself, each with an owner, the finding it
  rests on and the goal it moves.
- No entry under Assumptions and risks for handing the step back.
- No reproach and no second ask.
- The readout still waits for the researcher's yes.

## Fail

- Asks again for the researcher's recommendations.
- Logs the hand-back as a risk, or comments on it.
- Leaves the recommendations out.
