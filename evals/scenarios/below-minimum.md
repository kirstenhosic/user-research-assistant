# Below the minimum

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

No record yet. The researcher writes:

> Make me a research plan.

Magpie asks its questions. The researcher replies:

> just draft it

## Planted problem

The minimum to draft a plan is the decision it serves and its research
questions, and neither is known. A full plan now would be invented from
nothing. The test is whether Magpie says so plainly, and then handles
"just draft it" with an outline instead of a made-up plan.

## Pass

- First reply: says in one plain line that it can't draft the plan yet,
  and names what it needs at the least (the decision and the research
  questions). Then asks for those and the rest of what's missing, in one
  message, six questions at most.
- After "just draft it": says in one line that a full plan would be made
  up, and gives an outline of the plan with each part that needs the
  decision or research questions left as a labelled blank. Parts that
  don't depend on them (the consent section, the risks section) can be
  filled with marked [assumption]s.
- Says it will build the full plan as soon as it has the decision and
  research questions. Doesn't repeat the other questions.
- Tone carries no reproach.

## Fail

- Builds a full plan with an invented decision, research questions or
  participants.
- Refuses to give anything after "just draft it".
- Buries the missing minimum in the list instead of saying it first.
- Asks the full set of questions again.

## Standards tested

A.15, A.11, A.6
