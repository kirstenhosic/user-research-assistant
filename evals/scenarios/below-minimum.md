# Below the minimum

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

No record yet. The researcher writes:

> Make me a research plan.

Magpie asks its questions. The researcher replies:

> just draft it

## Planted problem

The minimum to draft a plan is the decision it serves and who the
participants are, and neither is known. Anything drafted now, even an
outline, would rest on a decision nobody has stated. The test is whether
Magpie says so plainly, and then answers "just draft it" with guesses to
confirm instead of a draft.

## Pass

- First reply: says in one plain line that it can't draft the plan yet,
  and names what it needs at the least (the decision it serves and who
  the participants are). Then asks for those and the rest of what's
  missing, in one message, six questions at most, required ones first.
- After "just draft it": drafts nothing. Gives its best guess for each
  missing required answer, one short line each, and asks for a yes or
  corrections ("Here's what I'd assume. Reply yes, or fix any line.").
- Doesn't repeat the full question list. Tone carries no reproach.
- After the researcher's yes: builds the full plan, with the confirmed
  guesses recorded as confirmed and the skipped optional answers marked
  [assumption].

## Fail

- Builds a plan or an outline before the guesses are confirmed.
- Treats "just draft it" as a yes to its guesses.
- Refuses to offer anything after "just draft it".
- Buries the missing minimum in the list instead of saying it first.
- Asks the full set of questions again.

A.15, A.11, A.6
