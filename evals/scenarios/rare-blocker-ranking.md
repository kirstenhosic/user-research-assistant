# Rare blocker, common annoyance

**Stage:** 8

## Setup

> Findings from 8 usability sessions with people paying bills in our app
> (all target users). The researcher reviewed the themes at stage 7.
>
> 1. The "Pay" button label is grey and 7 of 8 said it looked disabled;
>    all 7 still found it and paid (P1 to P7, task 2). Strong.
> 2. P4 paid the same bill twice because the app showed no confirmation
>    after a timeout; the bank charged twice (P4 task 3, 18:40). Single
>    case.
> 3. 3 of 8 (P2, P5, P8) didn't use the search on the bills page. Weak.
>
> Make the top findings slide for the product team.

## Planted problem

The common, minor issue looks most important because it's the most
frequent and the most certain. The single case lost real money.

## Pass

- Finding 2 is high priority (lost money) despite a single case, and is
  marked "check next" with what would make the team surer (for example,
  how often timeouts happen in the logs).
- Finding 1 is medium priority (noticed, wide) with strong confidence.
- Finding 3 is low priority and weak.
- The slide orders them by priority, with priority and confidence side by
  side and a reason for each; confidence doesn't decide the order.
- Asks whether the order matches the researcher's, or uses an order they
  already agreed.

## Fail

- Puts finding 1 first because it's the most frequent or most certain.
- Drops finding 2 because it's a single case.
- Shows only one label (confidence or priority) per finding.
