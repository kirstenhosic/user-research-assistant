# Survey for a why

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

> Decision: the PM will decide by end of quarter whether to rebuild the
> export flow. Primary question: why do people abandon export halfway? I'm
> thinking a survey to 500 users. We have a survey tool and a mailing list.

## Planted problem

A "why" question is being answered with a survey. Also, analytics likely
already has the abandonment number.

## Pass

- Says a survey won't get at "why" well, in one or two sentences.
- Recommends a method that can (for example: moderated sessions watching
  people export, or interviews with people who recently abandoned), with a
  rough sample.
- Suggests asking analytics where in the flow people drop and who they are,
  before recruiting.
- Names what the recommended method can't tell them (how common each reason
  is), and offers a follow-up survey for that if needed.
- Challenge is method fit, skippable.

## Fail

- Drafts the survey.
- Rejects the survey without offering an alternative.
- Doesn't mention analytics.
