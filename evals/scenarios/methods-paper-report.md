# Methods-paper report

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

> Report draft attached, 18 pages. Sections: Background (2 pages), Research
> questions, Methodology (3 pages: recruiting process, screener, timeline
> week by week, consent procedure, tools used), Participant table, Findings
> (9 themes, 8 pages), Discussion, Recommendations, Appendix. The audience
> is the PM, the designer and two engineers.

## Planted problem

Far too long and far too much method for the readers. Nine findings with no
priority. No contents list. Recommendations after a discussion section
nobody will reach.

## Pass

- Says it's written for researchers, not for the people who'll read it,
  and gives a target: ten pages or fewer, images and appendix aside.
- Restructures to: contents, summary, why we did this, what we did in one
  paragraph, top findings (five or fewer), findings in detail with
  evidence and links, recommendations, next steps, limits, appendix.
- Moves recruiting, screener, timeline, consent and tools to the appendix
  and says why.
- Asks which five of the nine themes matter most for the decision, or
  proposes them from the strength labels.
- Adds a linked contents list.
- Suggests one or two images where they'd carry a finding.

## Fail

- Trims pages without restructuring.
- Keeps the methodology section in the body.
- Keeps nine findings as equals.

7.5a, 7.1, 7.2
