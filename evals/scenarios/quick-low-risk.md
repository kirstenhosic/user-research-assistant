# Quick, low-risk study

**Stage:** 2 to 4

## Setup

The researcher writes:

> The growth team wants to know whether the new empty-state message on the
> reports page makes sense to people. They'll ship it behind a flag on
> Thursday either way and can switch it back in a day. I have until
> Wednesday and no budget. Can you plan something?

The product is live, the team can reach current customers through an
in-app prompt, and no participant incentive is available.

## Planted problem

A decision that is cheap and quick to undo, a very short deadline and no
budget. The test is that Magpie rates the risk as low with a reason and
sizes the study to match, instead of planning the full study it would run
with no limits, while still keeping it above the floor.

## Pass

- Asks for what's missing in one message (or builds with what it has if
  the researcher says so) and rates the decision risk low, with a reason
  tied to the flag and the one-day rollback.
- Recommends a small study that fits by Wednesday (for example, a handful
  of short sessions or a quick unmoderated test with current customers),
  and says in a line why that is enough for a low-risk decision.
- Has people do something with the message (find where to go next, say
  what they would do) rather than asking them to rate whether it's clear.
- Uses time-saving moves that keep rigor (one primary question, a short
  screener, sessions close together, results shared within a day or two).
- Lists any cut that weakens the answer under trade-offs with what it
  costs, such as no participants who have never seen a report.

## Fail

- Plans a large study, a long recruit or a survey for a decision the team
  can undo in a day, with no word on why that much is needed.
- Asks participants whether the message is clear or easy to understand,
  with no task.
- Names no decision risk, or rates it without a reason.
- Drops the trade-offs because the study is small.

## Standards tested

2.8, 3.9, 4.9
