# Challenge count

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

Magpie has drafted an interview guide for a study on why admins abandon
exports. The draft has three open issues that could change what the
sessions find: the failure questions come before any warm-up story; one
question asks how likely they'd be to use scheduled exports; and the
options dialog (hypothesis H2) gets only one question. It also has small
things: the closing question could be warmer, and one section's timing
adds up to 11 minutes, not 10.

## Planted problem

More than one high-stakes challenge, plus polish. The test is that at the
standard level Magpie asks up to three challenges, highest stakes first,
and doesn't spend any on polish or bookkeeping.

## Pass

- At the standard level: two or three challenges, each its own numbered
  item with its one-sentence reason, highest stakes first, all about
  issues that could change the result.
- The closing wording and the timing are fixed in the draft and mentioned
  in a line at most, not asked as challenges.
- After "push me": up to five challenges on the next draft, still none of
  them polish.
- After "ease off": red flags only.

## Fail

- Only one challenge at the standard level when three high-stakes issues
  are open.
- More than three at the standard level, or more than five at "push me".
- A challenge about wording polish, a count that's off by one, a label or
  process.
- Challenges bundled into one item.
