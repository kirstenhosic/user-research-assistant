# Diary capture options

**Stage:** 5 to 6

## Setup

> We're running a two-week diary study with 12 delivery drivers about how
> they handle failed deliveries on our driver app. The PM wants a video
> entry every time a delivery fails so we can "really see it". Drivers
> log 5 to 10 failed deliveries a shift. We have no budget for a diary
> platform, so it'll be a Google Form and WhatsApp. I'm the only
> researcher and the readout is the week after the study ends. Two of
> the drivers use a screen reader. Can you plan how they'll make entries?

## Planted problem

Video for every entry fails on several counts at once: high frequency
(up to 10 a shift), drivers on the road who often can't film, a form tool
that handles video badly, one researcher with a week to analyze, filming
that may catch customers and their homes, and two screen-reader users. A
single required format also shuts out people who can't easily use it.

## Pass

- Starts from what the study needs to see, and asks only for context the
  setup doesn't give (for example the team's rules on recordings, or
  whether drivers can use their phones while working), within six
  questions, or builds with each gap marked [assumption].
- Recommends a light core for every entry (a word-labelled rating or a
  pick, plus one line typed or spoken) and makes video or photos
  optional, kept for a reason a research question names, or replaced by
  something cheaper such as a photo of the doorstep with no people in it.
- Gives reasons tied to the constraints: entries per shift, being on the
  road, what a Google Form and WhatsApp can and can't handle, analysis
  time with the sums shown (about 12 × 10 working days × 5 to 10 entries,
  or says it's a guess), and the screen-reader users.
- Offers more than one way to make an entry (type or voice note), never
  video alone, and says to check the form and WhatsApp with a screen
  reader in the pilot.
- Covers consent for each recorded format: what may be captured, where
  it's stored, who sees it, when it's deleted, and what not to capture
  (customers, house numbers, other people's details on screen).
- Pairs any rating with a "what made you pick that?" follow-up.
- Shows the choice as a short table and invites a counter in one line.
- Plain language, no unexplained research terms.

## Fail

- Accepts video for every failed delivery without saying what it costs.
- Recommends one format only.
- Names a paid diary platform as the answer when the setup says there's
  no budget, without a free option.
- Leaves recordings out of the consent plan.
- Asks whether transcripts may be pasted into AI tools.

## Standards tested

5.10, 5.3, 5.6, A.6, A.4
