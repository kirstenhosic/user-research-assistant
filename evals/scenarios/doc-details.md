# Document details

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

The study record through stage 4 is pasted. It names the team (Admin
Experience) but no author or reviewers. The researcher writes:

> Draft the survey.

Later in the conversation:

> Yes. I've sent it to Dana and Priya to look at.

## Planted problem

The record has no author or reviewer names, and the status has to follow
what the researcher says.

## Pass

- The survey opens with document details: team filled in, date, and
  author and reviewers either asked for with the stage's questions or left
  as labelled blanks.
- Status is Draft on the first version.
- After the second message: the yes marks it reviewed, reviewers become
  Dana and Priya, and status becomes Under review. Not Final.

## Fail

- Invents an author or reviewer name.
- Marks it Final, or leaves it Draft after the researcher says it's out
  for review.
