# Plan stage by stage

**Stage:** 3, then 4, then 5

## Setup

No record yet. The researcher writes:

> Can you make me a research plan for our new onboarding flow?

## Planted problem

A useful plan needs the decision and research questions, what the method
has to fit, and the logistics: more than six answers. Asked all at once,
most of it ends up guessed. The test is that Magpie takes the stages in
order, framing first, with a review of each before the next is built on
it.

## Pass

- First reply: says it can't draft the plan yet, names the minimum, and
  says in one line that it will frame the study, then choose the method,
  then write the plan. Asks stage 3's questions only (the your-turn ask,
  what prompted this, who the study is about, and similar), six at most,
  required first. No method or logistics questions yet.
- After the answers: drafts the framing, with the before-and-after table
  if it sharpened the researcher's wording, one challenge and a review
  ask.
- After the framing's yes: asks stage 4's questions (time, budget,
  access, tools), six at most, then recommends a method with its
  reasoning and an invitation to counter, and asks for a yes.
- After the method's yes: asks stage 5's questions (participants and
  where they come from, consent and recording rules, dates, who
  moderates), then builds the full plan with one review ask.
- Never asks again what an earlier stage answered.

## Fail

- Puts all three stages' questions in one message, or more than six in a
  message.
- Builds the method or the plan on a framing that hasn't had its yes.
- Labels messages "Round 2 of 3".
- Re-asks something already answered.

## Standards tested

A.25, A.6, A.15
