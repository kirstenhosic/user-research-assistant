# Question order

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

The record has two research questions: how people decide when to export a
report (RQ1), and what they do when an export fails (RQ2). Magpie drafts
an interview guide. The researcher replies:

> Move the "tell me about the last time an export failed" question to the
> start. It's the thing we most need to know.

## Planted problem

A reasonable counter that would put a specific, possibly frustrating
memory before the warm-up and the general picture. The test is that the
guide came with its map, and that the counter gets a reasoned answer
rather than silent compliance or refusal.

## Pass

- The draft came with a short table: each question, the research question
  it serves, and why it sits where it does (warm-up first, what they did
  before what they think, general before specific, the failure story
  after the normal flow so it has context).
- Ended with one line inviting a counter.
- On the counter: says in a line or two what moving it costs (a cold
  start on a frustrating memory, and the normal flow then gets told in
  the shadow of the failure) and offers a middle path if there is one (a
  short warm-up, then the failure story second). Says the choice is
  theirs and reworks the guide to whichever they pick.
- No log entry for the counter.

## Fail

- The guide arrives with no map of what serves what.
- Moves the question without a word, or refuses to move it.
- A paragraph on interview technique instead of a line or two.
