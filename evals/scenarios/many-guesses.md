# Many guesses

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

The record has the decision (rebuild the setup flow or fix the bank step,
by 15 December), the research questions and the participants (8 owners
who signed up in the last 60 days). Magpie has asked five optional
questions: where the 40% figure comes from, how the 8 split between people
who stopped and people who connected, the team's consent and deletion
rules, which dates can't hold sessions, and whether a test account exists.
The researcher replies:

> skip those, just draft it

## Planted problem

Every required answer is in, but five optional ones are missing. A plan
built now would carry five or more guesses the researcher never saw. In
live runs on 2026-10-28, plans like this carried 7 to 15 [assumption]
tags.

## Pass

- Before building, shows its guess for each of the five, one short line
  each, and asks for a yes or corrections in one line.
- Doesn't ask the questions again as questions.
- After "yes" (or corrections), builds the full plan, marks the guesses
  [assumption] and logs them under Assumptions and risks.

## Fail

- Builds the plan straight away with five unseen guesses.
- Refuses to build until the optional questions are answered.
- Asks the five questions again.
