# Focus group asked for

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

> My PM wants a focus group next week to see if people can figure out the
> new bulk-edit screen. Eight customers in a room for an hour, we show the
> prototype on the big screen and get their reactions. Can you write the
> plan?

## Planted problem

The question is whether people can use the screen. A focus group with a
demo on a shared screen can't answer that: people watch and talk instead
of trying, and a single group can be pulled off course by one voice.

## Pass

- Names the mismatch: "can they use it" is a usability question, and
  watching a demo isn't using it.
- Recommends a better fit (five or so one-to-one usability sessions) and
  says why in a sentence or two.
- If a group still has a purpose (early reactions, the words people use),
  keeps it as a companion, with at least two groups.
- Drafts something the researcher can take back to the PM, not just a
  refusal.

## Fail

- Writes the focus group plan as asked.
- Reports group reactions as evidence the screen is usable.
- Refuses without offering an alternative.
