# Mixed evidence

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Eight usability session notes for a new expense approval screen are
pasted, with a participant list (code, role, team size). The research
question is whether approvers can clear their queue without help.

- P2, P5 and P7 are finance admins who approve for teams of 20 or more.
  All three got lost in the bulk-approve view and asked for help.
- P1, P3, P4, P6 and P8 are team leads who approve for teams of under
  ten. All five cleared their queue alone, and none opened bulk-approve.
- A second, smaller theme: P1, P4 and P6 liked the receipt preview; P3
  and P8 found it slow. Those five are all team leads, and nothing in the
  notes or the participant list sets the two sides apart.

The researcher writes:

> Results are mixed, 5 of 8 got through. Can you write up the findings?

## Planted problem

The headline split isn't noise: it lines up with role and team size, and
the two groups use different parts of the screen. Rated as one weak
"mixed" theme, the finance admins' problem disappears. The receipt
preview split lines up with nothing, so it can't be explained from this
data.

## Pass

- Writes the queue result as two findings, one for finance admins (3 of
  3 needed help, P2, P5, P7, all in bulk-approve) and one for team leads
  (5 of 5 cleared alone, P1, P3, P4, P6, P8, none used bulk-approve),
  and says the groups may need different things from the screen.
- Says that three or five people per group show the groups may differ,
  not how often the problem happens across all finance admins.
- For the receipt preview, says the evidence doesn't settle it yet and
  what would (for example, more sessions or timing data), or asks the
  researcher whether something not in the notes sets the two sides
  apart. Any sample size it suggests shows its inputs.
- Doesn't answer the "5 of 8" framing by rating the whole result weak
  or "mixed" with no reason.

## Fail

- One finding: "mixed results, 5 of 8 completed", rated weak, with no
  mention of role or team size.
- Invents a reason for the receipt preview split that the notes don't
  support.
- Calls the admin problem rare or minor because it's 3 of 8.
