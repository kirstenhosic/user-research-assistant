# Questions for you to answer

**Stage:** 3, the framing draft

## Setup

A new session. The researcher answers the stage 3 questions for a
market needs study: the Vault team will decide which new features to
prioritize "by January this year", choosing among top features in
competitor tools; the question that would help is "Which feature is most
exciting to the customer base right now?"; no team questions or
assumptions; the study is about end users and enterprise buyers; the
goal is revenue. They share a product link that can't be loaded.

Magpie drafts the framing. Two turns later, after the researcher says
"All looks good" and Magpie moves on to the method questions, the
researcher writes:

> What is the challenge, I missed it

## Planted problem

The framing reply needs four things from the researcher: a check on the
deadline year, an optional "go on" without waiting for the team's reply,
the challenge, and the review yes. The pull is to ask each where it comes
up in the draft (inside the Decision line, inside the summary paragraph),
so they're scattered and the challenge gets missed.

## Pass

- The framing reply ends with a `### Questions for you to answer`
  section, just before the status line, holding every ask, numbered:
  the year check, the challenge with its one-line reason, and the review
  ask last. A "go on" option, if offered, is in the section too.
- The draft above the heading holds no question marks addressed to the
  researcher and no "say X" asks; the deadline is marked [assumption]
  with the check in the section.
- The status line is short (`Magpie · Framing the study`, plus anything
  still open from an earlier reply) and doesn't list the new asks.
- The method-questions reply doesn't repeat the framing asks in full; its
  status line names what's still open from the framing (the challenge)
  in a few words.
- When the researcher asks what the challenge was, Magpie repeats it in
  full.
- The study record opens with "Questions for you to answer", holding the
  same open questions, and has no table of questions already asked.

## Fail

- Any ask outside the section, such as the year check inside the
  Decision line.
- No "Questions for you to answer" heading.
- The status line carries the asks instead of the section.
- An earlier question repeated in full in a later reply without the
  researcher asking for it.
