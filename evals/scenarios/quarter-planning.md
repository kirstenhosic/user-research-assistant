# Quarter planning

**Stage:** 1

## Setup

> I'm new and want to propose two or three studies for next quarter rather
> than wait for tickets. Here's the team profile [goals: grow self-serve
> revenue (trial-to-paid), reduce churn in year one; bets: a new reporting
> module; CUJs: set up workspace, build first report, share a report].
> Marketing has a buyer survey from March saying "integrations" is the top
> purchase driver. Support's top theme last quarter was "can't find my
> report". The library has one 2024 study on onboarding. What should I
> propose?

## Planted problem

None hidden; the test is whether Magpie builds opportunities from the
overlap of goals, market and user signals, labels sources correctly, and
attaches a decision and owner to each. The buyer survey is a trap: it's
about buyers, not users.

## Pass

- Lays out the three sides briefly: company goals and bets, market signal
  (the buyer survey, labeled partner data and indirect), user signals
  (support theme, the 2024 study with its date).
- Drafts three to five opportunities as one-liners, each naming the user
  need, the goal in the profile's own words, the market signal where it
  applies, and the gap. For example: "Report builders can't find reports
  they made, which touches year-one churn and the share-a-report CUJ;
  support sees it weekly; we don't know why or how often."
- Says the integrations signal is about buyers and asks what users do when
  integrations are missing, rather than proposing "integrations research"
  on the survey alone.
- Picks the top two or three with a plain-words reason, and for each names
  the decision it would inform and the likely owner.
- Offers to draft the opportunity briefs.

## Fail

- Produces a list of generic study ideas unconnected to the goals.
- Treats the buyer survey as a user finding.
- No decisions or owners.
- A scoring formula with invented numbers.
