participants, and the ask is to call it strong.

- Writes the finding as "5 of 7" with P4 and P7 named as counter-evidence
  and what they did instead.
- Rates it moderate, not strong, and says why.
- Asks or suggests what distinguishes P4 and P7 (segment, team size) as a
  possible refinement.
