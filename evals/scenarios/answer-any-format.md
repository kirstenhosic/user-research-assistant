# Answer in any format

**Stage:** 1 or 3, the first question message

## Setup

A new session: "Hi Magpie, can you help me with a study? I'm starting
from scratch and want help understanding market needs." After Magpie's
first questions, the researcher answers in one of three ways, each
holding the same facts (HashiCorp Vault; grow enterprise revenue; decide
by January 2027 which competitor features to prioritize; security
admins, platform engineers and app devs plus enterprise buyers; support
tickets and win/loss notes, no PRD; yes to a Product section):

- **A.** One paragraph, no numbers.
- **B.** Labeled lines ("Product: ...", "Decision: ...").
- **C.** Numbers that don't match Magpie's, with nested items, and
  "3. Yes please" meant for the Product section offer, which Magpie
  numbered differently.

## Planted problem

Researchers answer in their own order and numbering, as a real
researcher did in a live study. The pull is to match by number and
misplace answers, or to match by meaning silently so a wrong match goes
unnoticed.

## Pass

- The first questions section opens with one unnumbered line: answer in
  any order and any way, by number, by label or in a paragraph, and
  "skip 3" is fine.
- Every numbered item ends with "(required)" or "(optional)", written
  the same way, with optional items last.
- No numbered item holds a sub-list. If the stage 3 your-turn ask
  appears, it uses labeled lines ("Decision: ...").
- In all three cases every fact lands in the right place in the record.
- The next reply opens with a line saying what was taken from the
  answers. In case C it names the re-match ("I took your '3. yes please'
  as yes to the Product section").
- No question already answered is asked again (such as the year, given
  as 2027).

## Fail

- An answer placed under the wrong item because of its number.
- A re-match made without saying so.
- The "how to answer" line missing from the first section, or repeated
  in every reply.
- Labels missing, or written different ways ("Required.", "(Optional)").
