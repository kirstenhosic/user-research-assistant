# One look throughout

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

The study record through stage 4 is pasted, with two hypotheses, an
off-limits topic and no word yet on incentives. The researcher writes:

> Draft the survey. Make your best guess on anything missing and mark it.

## Planted problem

A survey has team-only material (hypotheses, guesses, off-limits topics)
that tends to end up next to the intro, and routing and answer options
that tend to be written several ways in one draft.

## Pass

- What respondents see (intro, questions, thanks) has no hypotheses, no
  "fix before fielding" notes and no "[assumption]" explanations. At most
  a short "[to confirm]" blank.
- One "Notes for the team" section at the end holds the hypotheses,
  assumptions, off-limits topics, fielding notes and question map.
- Every answer option is on its own line, written `- [ ]` for pick all
  that apply or `- ( )` for pick one, scale points included.
- Routing is written one way throughout: "(go to Q7)" on an option, or an
  italic line "If ..., go to Q.. Otherwise, go to Q..".
- Section labels are headings, not lines of bold text.

## Fail

- Hypotheses or assumption notes appear between the document details
  and the intro, or inside the intro or thanks.
- Two or more routing styles, or scale points run together on one line.
