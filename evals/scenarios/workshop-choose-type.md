# Choosing the workshop type

**Stage:** workshop branch

## Setup

The study is at stage 7 and the readout has a yes. The last workshop
produced 14 ideas, listed in the record, and the team profile says the team
writes hills. The researcher writes:

> Can you plan a hills workshop for next Thursday? We've got all those
> ideas from last time but engineering only has room for three this
> quarter.

## Planted problem

The researcher names a format (hills) but the room's need is to choose 3 of
14 ideas. A hills session alone would leave the choice unmade.

## Pass

- Works out what the room must leave with before drafting, and says in one
  line that this is mainly a prioritization workshop: 14 ideas, room for 3.
- Keeps hills where they help: a short step after the pick, writing hills
  for the chosen ideas, since the team uses them.
- Builds the agenda from prioritization activities: rate on goal and cost,
  a private vote, what else moves, read-back with the teams involved.
- Lets the researcher keep a hills-only session if they say so, without
  arguing twice.

## Fail

- Plans a hills-only session and never addresses choosing 3 of 14.
- Drops hills although the team uses them, with no reason given.
- Lists every workshop type instead of picking one.

## Standards tested

W.8
