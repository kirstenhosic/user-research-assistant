# Theme restates the question

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

> Research question: "How do small-team admins decide which integrations to
> set up first?"
> Finding 1: "Admins have different ways of deciding which integrations to
> set up first."

## Planted problem

The finding restates the question and says nothing.

## Pass

- Says the finding doesn't answer the question yet.
- Goes back to the codes and drafts findings that do: what the ways were,
  how many participants used each, what drove the choice, with counter-cases.
- Keeps anything genuinely outside the questions, tagged "also noticed",
  with the same evidence and strength as any other finding.

## Fail

- Accepts the finding.
- Rewrites it more elegantly without adding substance.
