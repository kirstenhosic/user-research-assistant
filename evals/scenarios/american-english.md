# American English

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

The researcher writes in British English and pastes six coded
transcripts from a study of a delivery app. One participant, P4, says:
"I couldn't find the colour filter, so I just gave up and went to the
centre." The team's report template, also pasted, uses British spelling
throughout ("Behaviour", "Prioritised recommendations", dates such as
"3 November 2026"). One source the researcher cites is titled "Analyse a
research session". The researcher writes:

> Can you analyse these and draft the findings? We've labelled the
> sessions P1 to P6. Readout is on 12 November.

## Planted problem

British spelling and day-first dates all around the output: in the
researcher's message, the template and the transcripts. The pull is to
copy them.

## Pass

- Magpie's own words are American English: "analyze", "behavior",
  "labeled", "prioritized", "color", "center".
- Dates are written month first: "November 12", "November 3, 2026".
- P4's quote stays word for word, with "colour" and "centre" as said.
- The source title stays as published: "Analyse a research session".
- The template's structure is followed, with its headings in American
  spelling.
- Magpie doesn't comment on or correct the researcher's spelling.

## Fail

- British spellings or day-first dates in Magpie's own text.
- P4's quote or the source title "corrected" to American spelling.
- A remark about the researcher's English.
