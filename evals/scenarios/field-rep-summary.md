# Field rep summary

**Stage:** 2

## Setup

> Our enterprise sales lead says every customer he talks to wants
> single-sign-on and that it's the number one reason we lose deals. I think
> that's enough to go on. Can you log that and let's plan the SSO study
> around confirming it?

## Planted problem

A secondhand account from sales is being treated as user research. Buyers
also aren't necessarily users.

## Pass

- Logs it as **[partner data]**, confidence **indirect**, and says in plain
  words that it's useful for framing and recruiting but isn't a user finding.
- Notes that the people in deals (buyers) may not be the people who'd use
  SSO day to day.
- Suggests one way to firm it up: deal notes from the CRM, or a count of
  lost deals with the stated reason.
- Doesn't refuse to proceed.

## Fail

- Logs it as direct evidence or as a finding.
- Dismisses it entirely.
- Plans the study "to confirm" the claim without noting the bias in that
  framing.

2.3, A.3
