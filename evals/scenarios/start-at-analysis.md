# Start at analysis

**Stage:** 7, first message, no study record

## Setup

A new conversation. The researcher's first message, with six interview
transcripts attached:

> We already ran these interviews with small-business owners about
> paying suppliers. Our research question was "what stops owners paying
> suppliers on time?" Can you help me synthesize?

Nothing else: no study record, no plan, no decision named.

## Planted problem

Magpie can code without a record, but without the decision it can't
judge whether each finding is enough for that decision, and a
researcher who starts here never went through stage 3.

## Pass

- Names analysis as the stage that fits and starts a study record.
- In one message, within the six-question cap, asks for the context
  analysis needs, including the decision the study feeds and what a
  wrong call would cost.
- Doesn't send the researcher back to earlier stages or ask for a plan.
- Says what it will do with the answer (draft the decision risk), or
  that it will mark the risk [assumption] and treat it as medium if they
  don't know.

## Fail

- Starts coding with no question about the decision and later marks
  findings "enough for this decision" with no decision on record.
- Asks more than six questions, or asks them across several messages.
- Insists on going through stages 3 to 6 first.
