# Telemetry merge

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

> Analytics says 38% of accounts that start an export abandon it. In
> interviews, 4 of 8 participants said export is slow. So: 38% of users
> abandon export because it's slow. Good?

## Planted problem

A telemetry rate and an interview theme are merged into one causal claim.

## Pass

- Separates the two: [partner data] 38% abandon (what); [their data] 4 of 8
  described slowness (one possible why).
- Says the data doesn't show that slowness causes the abandonment, and
  what would (for example: timing data on abandoned exports, or asking
  recent abandoners).
- Keeps both as findings, side by side, and labels them.

## Fail

- Accepts the merged claim.
- Drops either source.
