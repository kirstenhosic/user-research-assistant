# Asks clear from start to finish

**Stage:** 3 to 8, across a study

## Setup

A researcher runs a study with Magpie from framing to readout: the
framing with a summary to send to the team, the method with its
reasoning, the guide with its question map, analysis, and a readout with
drafted next steps. Their replies are short and answer only what's in
the questions section.

## Planted problem

A live walkthrough (2026-10-30) found asks that a researcher reading
only the questions section would miss or misread: "say 'go on'" and
"tell me if you have a template" in the body; "say if you'd order it
differently" under the question map; "question 5" meaning a guide
question in one line and a section item in the next; "What does X mean,
and what period...?" as one item; "Skip this if it's already handled";
made-up commands such as "say 'make the map'"; a required "What do you
want to change in the next steps?" next to the review yes; a reviewed
readout "still labeled Draft" with no word on how to make it final; and
full file paths.

## Pass

- No line above the questions section asks the researcher to say or do
  anything. Invitations to counter a method or an order, a template
  check and moving on are items in the section.
- At stage 3 the review item says the yes moves on to the method without
  waiting for the team's reply. There's no separate "say 'go on'".
- Draft items are named ("guide question 5", "RQ2", "finding 3") and
  section items are "item 3".
- Each numbered item asks one thing.
- The challenge says "Answer it, or reply 'skip' and I'll note it as a
  risk."
- Only words from the commands table are offered as things to say.
- The review item reads "Reply yes ..., or tell me what to change", and
  the next steps are named in the read-hardest line, not in their own
  item.
- After the readout's yes, one line says to reply "final" when it's
  ready to share.
- Files are named, not given as full paths.

## Fail

- Any of the planted problems appears.

## Standards tested

A.33, A.31, A.32, A.10
