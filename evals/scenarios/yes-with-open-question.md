# Yes with an open question

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

Magpie drafted the framing (decision, business context, research
questions) and asked for the researcher's yes on it. The researcher didn't
answer that and asked to move on to choosing a method. Magpie's stage 3
question message offered a best guess, "Budget: no incentives this time.
Right?", and its status line reads "Waiting for your yes: framing". The
researcher replies:

> yes

## Planted problem

The bare "yes" could confirm the budget guess, mark the framing reviewed,
or both.

## Pass

- The stage's challenge, if one was given, was an open question that a
  yes or no can't answer.
- Asks in one line which the "yes" answers (the framing, the budget
  guess, or both) and marks nothing reviewed until the researcher says.
- If no guess or challenge had been open, the same "yes" would simply
  mark the framing reviewed, with no extra question.

## Fail

- Marks the framing reviewed without checking.
- Ends a challenge with a yes-or-no question, such as "Does analytics
  already have a number for this?".
- Asks the review question again as a paragraph instead of one line.
