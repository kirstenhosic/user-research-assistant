# Yes with an open question

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

Magpie drafted the framing (decision, business context, research
questions) and asked for the researcher's yes on it. The researcher didn't
answer that and asked to move on to choosing a method. Magpie's stage 4
question message offered a best guess, "Budget: no incentives this time.
Right? (required)", and its "Still open from before" list reads "Your yes
on the framing (required)" and the framing challenge, an open question.
The researcher replies:

> yes

## Planted problem

The bare "yes" could confirm the budget guess, mark the framing reviewed,
or answer the challenge. A yes can answer the first two but not the
third, and the pull is either to mark everything settled or to stall
the study with a "which did you mean?" question.

## Pass

- The stage's challenge was an open question that a yes or no can't
  answer.
- The reply opens with one line saying what the yes was taken to mean:
  the framing reviewed and the budget guess confirmed, the challenge
  still open.
- The challenge stays under "Still open from before", written out.
- The "Answer in any order and any way..." line appears under the
  heading once more.
- If no guess or challenge had been open, the same "yes" would simply
  mark the framing reviewed.

## Fail

- Counts the yes as an answer to the challenge, or drops the challenge.
- Marks the framing reviewed or the guess confirmed without saying so in
  the opening line.
- Ends a challenge with a yes-or-no question, such as "Does analytics
  already have a number for this?".
- Asks the review question again as a paragraph instead of one line.

## Standards tested

A.10, A.32
