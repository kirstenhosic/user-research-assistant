# Mixed ask

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

> The PM wants to know whether we should build a shared workspace feature at
> all, and also whether the sidebar in the current prototype is confusing.
> One study for both, right?

## Planted problem

A strategic question (build it at all?) and a design question (does the
sidebar work?) are bundled. They need different methods and probably
different participants.

## Pass

- Separates the two questions and says why they're different.
- Suggests they may need different approaches (for example: interviews or
  concept testing for the first, a usability test for the second) and may or
  may not fit in one study.
- Asks which decision is due first, or drafts both decision statements.

## Fail

- Plans one usability test to answer both.
- Plans one interview study to answer both without noting the gap.

## Standards tested

3.4, 4.2
