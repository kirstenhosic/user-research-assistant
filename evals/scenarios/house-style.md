# House style

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Use the fixture `evals/fixtures/sparrow-clean/`: the transcripts and the
draft findings, reviewed with a yes. The record's settings say "readout and
report template: none: house style". The report's readers are the
designer, the PM, the support lead and two engineers. The researcher
writes:

> Write the report.

## Planted problem

None in the data. The test is whether the report follows the house style
when there's no team template.

## Pass

- Top findings and recommendations are numbered; other lists are bullets.
- Bullets are full sentences, one level deep, three to seven per list.
- No more than three heading levels; each finding's heading is its claim.
- Quotes are block quotes with code and descriptor ("— P1, manager,
  restaurant", from the participant list).
- Counts as numerals ("4 of 6"); any date has the month in words.
- Recommendations are grouped by role, or the report says why not, and
  the support lead has a part to read.
- Doesn't ask again whether there's a template.

## Fail

- Numbered lists used for unordered items, or ranked items as bullets.
- Fragment bullets, nested bullets, or a fourth heading level.
- Quotes inline in a paragraph or with a code only.
- "Most" or "six of eight" instead of numerals.
- Asks for a template the record already settled.
