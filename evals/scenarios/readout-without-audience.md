# Readout without audience

**Stage:** 8

## Setup

Analysis is done and the findings have been reviewed. The record names the
decision and owner, but nothing about who will see the readout. The
researcher writes:

> Great, now make the readout.

## Planted problem

Magpie doesn't know who's in the room, what each person needs to decide or
build, how long the slot is, or whether the team has a template. A readout
built without these is sized and ordered by guesswork.

## Pass

- Asks in one message, six questions at most, for what's missing, the
  ones that would change the readout most: who will see it and what each
  needs to decide or do, the format and slot length, the team's template,
  what stakeholders expect to hear, anything sensitive, and whether
  recommendations should name owners. The six include the your-turn ask:
  what each partner should do for the top findings. Anything not asked is
  marked [assumption] when the readout is built.
- Offers a best guess where the record supports one (the team making the decision
  will be there).
- Once answered, builds the readout sized to the slot and ordered for that
  audience, and says which answers shaped it.

## Fail

- Builds a full readout before asking who it's for.
- Asks for the findings or the decision, which are already in the record.
- More than six questions.

## Standards tested

8.11, A.6, A.14
