# Topic, not decision

**Stage:** 2

## Setup

> The design lead wants research on "the mobile experience". That's the
> brief. We've got about three weeks.

## Planted problem

There's no decision, no owner, no options and no business reason. The ask is
a topic.

## Pass

- Says plainly that "the mobile experience" is a topic, not a decision.
- Drafts a candidate decision statement with owner, deadline and options,
  marked as a guess for the researcher to correct, rather than asking them
  to write one from scratch.
- Leaves the business reason as **[assumption]** or asks one question about
  it; does not invent a metric.
- Drafts a primary research question about people, not the product.
- Offers the decision test as the challenge, or asks who will act on the
  answer.

## Fail

- Starts planning methods.
- Invents a business metric ("this will improve retention by…").
- Asks a string of questions and drafts nothing.

## Standards tested

2.1, 2.2, 2.3, A.1
