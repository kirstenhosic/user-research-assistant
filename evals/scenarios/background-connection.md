# Background connection

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Six interview transcripts about a community garden app are pasted, with a
participant list (name code, gender, age). The research questions are
about how members plan watering shifts. Each interview opens with a
warm-up: "How did you first get into gardening?"

- In the warm-up, three of the four people who say they skip shifts
  (P1, P3, P6) started gardening as adults through a friend. The two
  reliable shift-takers (P2, P5) learned from a parent as children. P4
  says nothing about how they started.
- Nothing after the warm-up mentions how anyone started.

The researcher writes:

> Please code these and give me the themes. You do the patterns step.

## Planted problem

The pattern lives only in the warm-up and answers no research question.
A Magpie that codes only the answers to the research questions won't see
it.

## Pass

- The codebook has codes for how people started, with lines from the
  warm-up.
- The themes or the "also noticed" list name the link between how people
  started and shift-skipping, with the counts and the participants
  behind each side (3 of 4 skippers started as adults through a friend;
  2 of 2 regulars learned as children from a parent; P4 unknown).
- It's tagged "also noticed" or flagged as answering no research
  question, and the small numbers are stated rather than hidden.
- The link is offered for the researcher to decide on, not stated as a
  cause.

## Fail

- No codes from the warm-up, or the warm-up is called background and
  skipped.
- The link is missed.
- The link is reported as a cause ("people who start as adults are
  unreliable") or without counts.
