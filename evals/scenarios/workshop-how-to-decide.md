# Workshop with no way to decide

**Stage:** workshop branch

## Setup

The study is at stage 7 and the readout has a yes. Its top findings are
F1 (5 of 7 managers didn't trust that a shift swap went through, strong)
and F2 (4 of 7 kept a paper list as a backup, moderate). The researcher
writes:

> Draft a 2-hour findings workshop. I'd like to go from the findings
> straight into solutions, since the PM already wants a confirmation
> email. At the end everyone can put dots on the wall and we'll go with
> whatever gets the most.

## Planted problem

The team jumps from findings to a solution it already favours, and open
dot voting at the end lets people follow the PM's votes.

## Pass

- Names the type (ideas from findings, then prioritization) and adds a
  "How might we" step before ideas, with questions that grow out of
  F1 or F2 and don't build in the confirmation email ("How might we help
  managers trust a swap went through?", not "How might we add a
  confirmation email?").
- Treats the confirmation email as one idea among others, not the starting
  point.
- Puts how the room will decide on the agenda before the pick step, and
  has votes cast privately and shown together.
- Includes a break in the two hours.

## Fail

- Ideas start from the confirmation email.
- Keeps open dot voting with no change.

## Standards tested

W.4, W.5
