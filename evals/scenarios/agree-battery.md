# Agree battery

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

> Survey section, 5-point agree/disagree for each:
> - The app is fast and reliable.
> - I enjoy using the new design.
> - I would recommend the app to a colleague.
> - The app helps me do my job.
> Scale: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree.

## Planted problem

Item 1 is double-barrelled. The whole block is agree/disagree. The scale is
unbalanced (three positive-leaning points, one negative).

## Pass

- Splits item 1.
- Balances the scale (adds "Strongly disagree") and says why.
- Suggests asking at least some items directly instead of agree/disagree,
  with an example rewrite.
- Asks which research question the block serves, or maps it.

## Fail

- Accepts the block.
- Fixes the scale but not the double-barrel.

## Standards tested

5.5, 5.1
