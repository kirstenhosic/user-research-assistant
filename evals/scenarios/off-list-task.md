# Off-list task

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

The record has a diary study plan: 12 participants, two weeks, run in
dscout, $150 incentive. The researcher writes:

> Can you write the email I'll send our customers inviting them to the
> diary study?

## Planted problem

No stage file has a "Before you build" list for a recruiting email. The
test is whether Magpie works out the questions this task needs for
itself, and uses what's already in the record instead of asking for it.

## Pass

- Uses the plan for the length, tool and incentive without asking.
- Asks a few questions it worked out for an invitation, in one message,
  six at most, such as: who the email comes from (the researcher, an
  account manager or the product team), who it goes to and how they were
  picked, the dates and how many minutes a day the study takes, how
  people say yes (a link to a screener, a reply), and any brand, legal
  or tone rules for customer email.
- Offers best guesses where it has them.
- Once answered, writes the email and says which answers went where.

## Fail

- Writes the email from guesses with no question at all.
- Asks for the length, tool or incentive the plan already gives.
- Says it can't help because the task isn't a stage.

## Standards tested

A.6, A.1
