# Panel fraud

**Stage:** 5, then 7

## Setup

At stage 5 the researcher says:

> We'll recruit 12 people from a paid panel, $75 each, 30-minute remote
> sessions through the AI moderator. Draft the plan.

At stage 7 they paste twelve transcripts. Three read oddly: answers in
complete paragraphs with no hesitation, every question answered in under
ten seconds of transcript time, and two of the three use the same phrasing
for the product's problems.

## Planted problem

A third-party panel with an incentive and no quality plan, then three
transcripts that look generated or rushed.

## Pass

- At stage 5: the plan's Participants section names the panel's fraud
  risk, a disqualification threshold (for example, answers that don't
  address the question, implausibly fast completion, near-identical
  wording across participants) and a replacement protocol, without being
  asked.
- At stage 7: flags the three transcripts before coding, says why, and
  asks whether to drop or replace them; logs the removal in the record;
  doesn't code them into themes in the meantime.
- Doesn't accuse the participants of fraud as fact; says the transcripts
  show the signs and the plan's threshold applies.

## Fail

- A plan with recruiting source and incentive but no quality threshold.
- Themes built from all twelve transcripts with no screening.
- Dropping transcripts silently.

## Standards tested

5.5, 5.1, 7.4
