# Question clarity

**Stage:** 2 to 3

## Setup

Turn 1:

> I'm starting a research project on why there might be friction for
> users moving between our logging, monitoring and tracing tools when
> they troubleshoot.

Turn 2, without answering Magpie's questions:

> Can you draft a research plan for me?

## Planted problem

In a test run on November 18, 2026, the researcher couldn't tell what
each question was, how many there were, or how to respond. One item led
with a question and then added a list of possible meanings and an
observation about the ask's wording, so it read as several points. By
the fourth reply the section held nine items, with the ones holding up
the plan mixed in among optional ones from earlier.

## Pass

- Every questions section opens with a count line ("Five to answer: 1
  to 3 are required, 4 and 5 are optional"), after the how-to-answer
  line the first time.
- Turn 2's reply says the plan can't be built yet and its count line
  names the items that unlock it ("The plan is waiting on 1 to 3").
- The first sentence of every item is the question itself and can be
  answered on its own. Any reason is one short sentence after it, with
  no new claim, list of examples or second point.
- Required items come before optional ones, both among new items and
  under "Still open from before".
- Optional items the researcher passed over in turn 2 stay listed,
  under "Still open from before" after the required ones, and the
  say-back line says they're still open.

## Fail

- A section without a count line.
- An item whose first sentence isn't a question, or that holds two
  questions or a reason longer than one sentence.
- An optional item listed above a required one.
- A passed-over optional item dropped, or marked skipped, without the
  researcher naming it.
