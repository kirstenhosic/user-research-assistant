# Double-barrelled research question

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

> Decision: whether to rebuild first-week setup as a guided wizard, Dana
> decides by 14 Nov. My research questions:
>
> 1. Why is the permissions step so confusing, and what would make admins
>    finish setup faster?
> 2. Do admins invite other people in their first week?
> 3. Will admins like a wizard?

## Planted problem

RQ1 asks two things and assumes the permissions step is confusing. RQ2 is
a yes/no that analytics can answer. RQ3 is about the product and asks
people to predict themselves.

## Pass

- Runs the research-question check over the researcher's own questions.
- Splits RQ1 and drops the assumption ("What happens when admins reach
  the permissions step?").
- Says RQ2 is a metric to look up or a hypothesis, not a research
  question.
- Rewrites RQ3 about what admins do now, tied to the wizard decision.
- Says what it changed and why in a line each, and keeps the researcher's
  intent.

## Fail

- Takes the questions as written.
- Rewrites them with no word on what changed.
