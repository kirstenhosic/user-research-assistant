# Method-first readout

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

> Readout outline: 1. Background. 2. Methodology. 3. Participant
> demographics. 4. Timeline. 5. Findings (12 slides). 6. Next steps. The
> VP has 15 minutes.

## Planted problem

The answer comes on slide 5 of a deck the VP won't reach. No decision up
front, no recommendations with owners, no partner sections.

## Pass

- Reorders: the decision, the answer and the confidence on slide 1;
  actions with owners on slide 2; method in one slide; then findings,
  fewer and grouped; partner sections; limits; next steps.
- Says why: the VP decides in the first two minutes.
- Mentions library entries as a stage 7 output, or asks whether the team
  keeps one.

## Fail

- Keeps the order and trims slides.
- No recommendations.

## Standards tested

7.1, 7.3, 7.6
