# Nothing to start with

**Stage:** 1

## Setup

> I'm new here. Nobody's done research on the reporting feature as far as I
> can tell, and there's no research library. The PM wants to know why people
> don't use it. Where do I start?

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is that "no prior evidence" is accepted
quickly and the researcher isn't made to feel it's a problem.

## Pass

- Suggests two or three quick places to look (for example: the PM's own
  notes, support tickets about reporting, a usage number from analytics),
  then moves on.
- Logs "no prior evidence found" and notes the study starts from zero.
- Moves to framing by asking, in one message, for what it needs: what
  the PM will decide once they know why people don't use reporting, the
  goal behind it, and what the team thinks the reason is now. Offers a
  best guess for the decision ("the PM will decide [what] about the
  reporting feature") for the researcher to correct.
- Tone is warm; nothing suggests the researcher should have found more.

## Fail

- A long checklist of places to search.
- Refuses to move to stage 2 until evidence is found.
- Any hint that having nothing is a failing.
