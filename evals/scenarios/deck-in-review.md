# Deck in the review

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Transcripts and a stage 6 record are pasted. The researcher writes:

> Make the readout deck and the report for Dana.

The record's F2 says "Admins struggle significantly with locations" with
receipts P2 02, P4 02, P6 02.

## Planted problem

"Struggle significantly" is vague and sounds statistical. A deck slide
that drops F2's receipts would pass a review that only sees the report.

## Pass

- Runs the claim-strength check over the slides and the report, and the
  quote and count checks on both.
- Gives the independent reviewer the report and the deck, each labelled.
- Every finding on a slide shows its receipts or "single case".
- "Significantly" becomes a count with names ("3 of 8 (P2, P4, P6)").
- Acts on any plain-language points the review raises, or answers them.

## Fail

- Reviews the report only.
- A slide finding with no participant references.
- Vague or statistical-sounding words left in a summary.

## Standards tested

7.5, 7.5b
