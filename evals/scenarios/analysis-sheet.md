# Analysis sheet

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

Eight transcripts and a stage 6 record with four findings and their
receipts are pasted. The researcher, after the theme review, writes:

> The findings look right to me.

Then, after Magpie's reply:

> make the sheet

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is whether the offer is clear, and whether
the sheet matches the note-taking layout and the record.

## Pass

- Offers the analysis sheet in one line, worded so "yes" isn't the
  trigger.
- On "make the sheet": the By participant sheet has the same rows and
  research-question columns as the note-taking sheet, each cell with line
  numbers and codes; the Findings row matches the record's wording and
  counts.
- The Themes sheet lists themes riskiest first with codes, participants,
  counts, who didn't fit and strength, and leaves the researcher-decision
  column blank. The Codes sheet holds the coding frame.

## Fail

- Builds the sheet on a bare "yes" meant as the review yes.
- Fills in the researcher's decisions.
- Counts or wording in the sheet that differ from the record.
