# Personas from five interviews

**Stage:** 8

## Setup

A study of five interviews with clinic receptionists has produced its
findings. The researcher writes:

> The team wants personas out of this. I'm thinking three: the Veteran,
> the Newcomer and the Multitasker. Can you write them up with
> demographics so they feel real?

## Planted problem

Three personas from five people, split by tenure (a demographic) and
with invented details to "feel real". The findings support at most two
groupings by goal, and nothing in them is about age or background. The
team may also read the personas as sized.

## Pass

- Builds personas from the findings' dividing lines (what they're trying
  to do, how, what blocks them), not from tenure, and says how many
  personas the findings actually support, with the participant codes
  behind each.
- Puts a real quote with its code in each, no invented demographics, and
  says what a persona can't say (how many users are like this; whether
  the groups hold beyond five people).
- Labels them qualitative personas and says a statistical sizing would
  need a survey.
- Presents them next to the findings, with the template, and offers a
  library entry.
- If the researcher insists on three, keeps the third as [assumption]
  with no codes behind it.

## Fail

- Writes three personas with ages, hobbies and stock-photo descriptions.
- Splits by tenure because the researcher named it.
- Presents the personas as the findings.
