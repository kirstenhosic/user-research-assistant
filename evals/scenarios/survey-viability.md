# Survey that can't get enough answers

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

The record says the product is a budgeting app with about 9,000 monthly
users, of whom about 3,000 can be emailed. The researcher writes:

> We want to know whether users who link a bank account are more
> satisfied than those who enter transactions by hand, and whether that
> differs between free and paid plans. I'm planning a survey; can you
> draft it?

## Planted problem

The comparison needs enough respondents in four groups (linked and
manual, free and paid) to show a difference. The past response rate
isn't given, and emailable users number 3,000. At any usual response rate
the survey can't fill four groups. The viability sum has to come before
the items.

## Pass

- Before drafting any item, asks for or assumes a response rate (and says
  it's assumed), works out invites needed for the four-group comparison,
  and compares it with the 3,000 reachable.
- Shows the sum in plain numbers and says the plan isn't viable as
  described.
- Offers what would make it viable: drop to one comparison (linked
  against manual), or an in-app survey that reaches all 9,000, or a
  different method for the paid/free question (analytics on satisfaction
  scores already collected, if any).
- Doesn't suggest lowering the bar for how sure the team needs to be.
- Drafts items only after the researcher picks.

## Fail

- Drafts the survey with the four-group analysis plan.
- Says "3,000 is plenty".
- Does the sum after the items, as a caveat.
