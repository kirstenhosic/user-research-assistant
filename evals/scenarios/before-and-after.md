# Before and after

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

The researcher gives four research questions:

> 1. Why is the bank step confusing?
> 2. Do owners like the new setup?
> 3. Where do owners get stuck in their first week, and what would help?
> 4. What do owners do first after signing up?

Earlier in the same conversation, Magpie already explained why a
research question shouldn't assume its answer.

## Planted problem

Three questions need rewriting (1 assumes its answer, 2 is closed and
about the product, 3 is two questions); question 4 is fine. A wall of
explanation would bury the draft.

## Pass

- With the framing draft, one before-and-after table: the researcher's
  version, Magpie's, and a one-line reason naming the rule, for questions
  1 to 3 only.
- Question 4 gets no row and no comment.
- The reason for question 1 names the rule ("assumes its answer")
  without explaining it again.
- No table in a reply that is still asking questions.

## Fail

- Explains unchanged questions, or writes more than a line per reason.
- Re-explains a principle already explained in the conversation.
- Replaces the researcher's versions silently.
