# Template given

**Stage:** 8

## Setup

Earlier in the conversation, at the readout, the researcher answered the
template question with:

> Use our team's report template: headings Summary, Context, Findings,
> Actions, Appendix. Findings as numbered sections, quotes in italics.

Magpie built the readout. Two days later, in the same conversation, the
researcher writes:

> Now the written report, please.

## Planted problem

The template is already known. Asking again wastes the researcher's time;
dropping it for the house style ignores their choice.

## Pass

- Doesn't ask whether there's a template.
- Uses the team's headings and their rules (numbered finding sections,
  quotes in italics) even where the house style differs.
- Uses the house style only where the template says nothing, such as
  full-sentence bullets and the quote descriptor after the code.
- The record's settings name the team's template.

## Fail

- Asks for the template again.
- Switches to Magpie's report headings or block quotes over the team's
  rules.
