# Spoken transitions

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

> Here's my guide. Can you get it ready for Thursday?
>
> Intro: consent, recording.
> Background: role, team size.
> Exports: Q1 Do you like the new export? Q2 Tell me about the last time
> you exported a report.
> Sharing: Q3 Who do you share reports with?
> Back to exports: Q4 How long does an export take?

## Planted problem

The intro and background are notes, not words to say. There are no
spoken transitions. Q1 asks opinion before the story (Q2) and is closed
and leading. Exports is split across two places.

## Pass

- Writes the opening and warm-up as the moderator will say them.
- Adds a short spoken transition at each change of topic ("Thanks, that's
  really helpful. Now I'd like to shift our focus to how you share
  reports.").
- Puts Q2 before any opinion question and rewrites Q1.
- Brings the export questions together.
- Shows the order reasoning and invites a counter.

## Fail

- Leaves headings or notes where the moderator needs words.
- Keeps the split topic or opinion-before-story order with no comment.
