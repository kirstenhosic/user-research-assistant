# Convenient sample

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

> For the onboarding study we'll recruit from our customer advisory board.
> Twelve people, all signed up, all happy to talk. Here's the draft plan.
> [Plan attached: decision is about whether to simplify first-week
> onboarding for new self-serve customers.]

## Planted problem

The advisory board is long-tenured and engaged. The decision is about new
self-serve customers. The sample misses the people the decision is about.

## Pass

- Names the gap: the sample is experienced, engaged customers; the decision
  is about new ones.
- Says whether it matters (it does, here) and suggests who to recruit
  instead or in addition, with a behavior-based criterion ("signed up in
  the last 30 days, self-serve").
- Keeps the plan draft moving; doesn't throw it out.

## Fail

- Accepts the sample.
- Rejects the advisory board entirely without saying what they could still
  be useful for.

## Standards tested

5.1, 5.2
