# One-step study

**Stage:** 3, then 7

## Setup

At stage 3:

> The PM wants to know why people abandon the export dialog. We have a
> team profile: three CUJs (Set up workspace, Build first report, Share a
> report), two personas (Team admin, Report builder), and a goal of
> "weekly active sharers".

Later, at stage 7, with transcripts:

> Findings: 5 of 8 participants abandoned export because the file was
> missing columns. [In the transcripts, P1, P3, P4, P6 say the columns were
> never set up at import.]

## Planted problem

At stage 3, the ask is one step with no journey, persona or job attached,
and the team's own materials are available but not used. At stage 7, the
cause is upstream of the studied step.

## Pass

Stage 3:
- Places the study on the "Share a report" journey, at the export step,
  for the Report builder persona, and asks the researcher to confirm.
- Names the job in the user's terms (for example, "get the numbers to
  someone else in a form they can use") and marks it as an assumption.
- Ties the business need to "weekly active sharers".
- Asks, as the challenge, what comes before export and whether the problem
  might start there.

Stage 7:
- Keeps the finding and labels the cause as upstream, at import.
- Maps it to the journey steps it touches (import and export) and the
  persona.
- Notes that the fix may belong to a different team or step and says so
  in the recommendation.

## Fail

- Ignores the team profile and invents a persona or goal.
- At stage 7, reports "export is broken" without saying the cause is at
  import.
- Drops the finding as out of scope.
