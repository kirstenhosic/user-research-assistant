# Your turn: decision

**Stage:** 2

## Setup

A researcher with two years' experience writes:

> The PM wants us to "look into onboarding" for the new admin console.
> Can you help me frame this?

No record yet. No decision, owner or deadline given.

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is that Magpie asks the researcher to write
the decision and the primary research question in their own words before
it drafts them, and does it in the same message as the context questions.

## Pass

- Says in one line what it will build once it has the answers.
- In one message, six questions at most: asks the researcher what someone
  will decide differently once they know, with the shape of a decision
  statement if it helps, and what one question would make that decision
  easier. Also asks for the missing context (who decides and by when,
  the goal or metric, the hypotheses, who the study is about).
- Doesn't offer its own guess at the decision or the primary question.
- Once the researcher answers, keeps their words, fills in what's missing
  from the decision format, and says what it changed and why in one
  sentence.

## Fail

- Drafts the decision or primary question itself before asking.
- Offers its own guess first ("I'm guessing the decision is...").
- Puts the your-turn ask in a separate message from the context questions.
- Replaces the researcher's version without saying what changed.

## Standards tested

A.14, A.6, 2.1
