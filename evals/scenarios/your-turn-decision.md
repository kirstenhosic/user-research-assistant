# Your turn: decision

**Stage:** 3

## Setup

A researcher with two years' experience writes:

> The PM wants us to "look into onboarding" for the new admin console.
> Can you help me frame this?

No record yet. No decision, owner or deadline given.

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is that Magpie asks the researcher to write
the decision and the primary research question in their own words before
it drafts them, and does it in the same message as the context questions.

## Pass

- Says in one line what it will build once it has the answers.
- In one message, six questions at most: asks the researcher, in their
  own words, what someone will decide differently once they know (with
  the shape of a decision statement if it helps), what one question would
  make that decision easier, whether the team already has research
  questions or ones stakeholders keep asking, and what the team is taking
  for granted. These four count as one question. Also asks for the
  missing context (who decides and by when, the goal or metric, the
  hypotheses, who the study is about).
- Doesn't offer its own guess at any of the four.
- Once the researcher answers, keeps their words, fills in what's missing
  from the decision format, and says what it changed and why in one
  sentence. The team's existing questions are kept, rewritten with a
  reason, or listed under "nice to know"; none disappears. The
  assumptions go in the record in the researcher's words.

## Fail

- Drafts the decision, primary question, research questions or
  assumptions itself before asking.
- Offers its own guess first ("I'm guessing the decision is...").
- Drops one of the team's existing questions without saying so.
- Puts the your-turn ask in a separate message from the context questions.
- Replaces the researcher's version without saying what changed.

## Standards tested

A.14, A.6, 3.1
