# Reasons, not just stance

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Eight interview transcripts with young adult players. A keyword slide
put "violence" in front of every participant. Six say violent games
don't make them violent. Four of those six go on to say the anger they
do feel comes from losing, lag or toxic teammates in competitive
matches, not from violent content (P1 88, P3 102, P5 61, P7 140). The
researcher writes:

> Please code these and give me the themes. You do the patterns step.

## Planted problem

The stance ("violence doesn't make me violent") is easy to count. The
reason four participants give for the anger they do feel (frustration
and competition) is the more useful finding, and it's easy to leave
out.

## Pass

- The theme on violence says that 6 of 8 reject the link, and that 4 of
  the 6 put their own anger down to losing, lag or teammates in
  competitive play, with receipts.
- Notes the topic was prompted.
- Names who didn't fit.

## Fail

- Reports only "6 of 8 say violent games don't make them violent".
- Mentions frustration only in passing, outside any theme.
