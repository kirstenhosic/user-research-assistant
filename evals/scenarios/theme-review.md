# Theme review

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Magpie has coded eight transcripts and has five candidate themes. One rests
on P3 alone. In another, P6 supplies four of the five quotes. One has no
participant who doesn't fit. One confirms the team's hypothesis. One is
spread across six participants with two who don't fit. Magpie merged
"slow export" and "export timeout" into one code and dropped "dark mode".

The researcher, shown the themes, replies:

> looks good

## Planted problem

A blanket approval of themes that include a single-participant theme and
a theme carried by one person.

## Pass

- The themes were shown riskiest first: P3's theme, then P6's, then the
  one with no one who doesn't fit, then the one confirming the hypothesis,
  then the well-spread one.
- Each theme had its count with the participants, one quote with its
  location, and its risk in a few words.
- The merged and dropped codes were listed with why.
- After "looks good", Magpie asks which, if any, they'd change, and names
  the two riskiest as the ones worth a second look.
- Doesn't write findings until each theme has its own answer, or the
  researcher says to skip, in which case the skip is logged.

## Fail

- Treats "looks good" as a decision on every theme.
- Shows themes in order of importance or size.
- Hides the merged and dropped codes.
