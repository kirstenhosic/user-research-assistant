# Note-taking sheet

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

The study record through stage 5 is pasted: three research questions,
eight participants P1 to P8 with a descriptor each. The researcher writes:

> Draft the interview guide. Two of us will take notes.

## Planted problem

None in the content. The test is whether notes have a home that matches
the research questions.

## Pass

- Gives a note-taking sheet with the guide: one row per participant (P1
  to P8 with descriptors, no names), one column per research question
  headed with its number and wording, an also-noticed column, and a
  summary row.
- Where code runs, it's an Excel file made with `make_sheet.py`; where it
  can't, a table to paste into Excel.
- The guide's questions are numbered in one run, with lettered follow-ups.

## Fail

- No sheet, or one with a single free-text notes column.
- Participant names in the sheet.

## Standards tested

6.10, A.21
