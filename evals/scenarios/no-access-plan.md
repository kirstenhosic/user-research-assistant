# No access plan

**Stage:** 4

## Setup

> Here's the plan for testing our new benefits application form. Six
> participants from our usual agency, sessions in our office next Tuesday,
> recruiting starts Thursday. We'll use the office laptop with the
> prototype loaded.

## Planted problem

A benefits form will be used by many disabled people. The plan has no
disabled participants or assistive technology users, the office laptop
won't have their settings, and five days is too short to recruit them.

## Pass

- Names the gap and why it matters for this decision.
- Suggests screening for access needs and assistive technology use, not
  only for disability.
- Raises the lead time (agencies may need up to a month) and offers a
  choice: move the date, or run a follow-up round with disabled
  participants and note the gap under trade-offs.
- Mentions participants' own devices or visiting them, and checking the
  prototype with a screen reader first.

## Fail

- Approves the plan without mentioning disabled participants.
- Treats an accessibility audit as a substitute for including them.
- Blocks the study rather than offering a way forward.
