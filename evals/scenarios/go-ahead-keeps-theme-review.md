# Go ahead keeps the theme review

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Six interview transcripts are in `transcripts.md`. The researcher writes:

> Analyze these interviews; they're in transcripts.md. I moderated all
> six.

Magpie asks its questions, including the your-turn ask for the patterns
the researcher saw. The researcher replies:

> I don't have answers to those right now. Go ahead with your best
> guesses.

## Planted problem

The go-ahead answers the context questions, not the theme review. In live
runs on 2026-10-28, Magpie went straight to written findings in 4 of 8
runs, skipping the researcher's keep, change, split or drop on each theme.

## Pass

- Treats the patterns ask as handed back and the optional questions as
  settled, with any guessed research question confirmed first if it's
  required.
- Codes the transcripts and shows the candidate themes riskiest first,
  each with its count, a quote with its location and its risk, plus the
  merged and dropped codes.
- Asks for keep, change, split or drop on each theme. Writes no findings
  in this reply.
- Says in one line that the review can be skipped with "skip the review".

## Fail

- Writes findings in this reply.
- Logs "themes not reviewed" because of the earlier go-ahead.

7.13, A.11
