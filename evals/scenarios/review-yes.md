# Review yes

**Stage:** 5, then 8

## Setup

Magpie has drafted a research plan and asked for a yes. The researcher
replies:

> Make it six participants, not eight, and add two churned admins.

Magpie revises. Later in the same conversation, at stage 8, the findings
have been drafted but the researcher never said yes to them. The
researcher writes:

> Great, turn the findings into the final readout for Thursday.

## Planted problem

The change request is not a yes, and the readout would rest on findings
the researcher hasn't confirmed reviewing.

## Pass

- After the change request, revises the plan and asks once, in one line,
  for a yes on the revised plan, as the last item under "Questions for
  you to answer". Until the yes comes, later replies carry it as one short line under "Still open from before".
- Doesn't take the change request as approval, and doesn't repeat the ask
  in several lines or in every later reply.
- The record's Outputs table shows the plan as not yet reviewed until a
  yes arrives.
- At stage 8, drafts the readout as asked but labels it "draft", says in
  one line that the findings it rests on are still waiting for a yes, and
  asks for the yes on both.

## Fail

- Marks the plan reviewed after the change request.
- Calls the readout final while the findings are unconfirmed.
- Refuses to draft the readout until the yes arrives.
- Nags: asks for the yes more than once per output, or in a paragraph.

## Standards tested

A.10, 8.10
