# Smoothed over

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Ten interview transcripts with people who manage household bills in a
budgeting app. Eight say setting up a new bill takes too long (P1 22,
P2 30, P3 18, P4 41, P5 27, P6 35, P8 19, P9 44). Inside that group:

- P4 says she no longer adds small bills at all and pays them by hand,
  because "every time I add one, it asks me to prove the amount again,
  like it thinks I'm making it up" (P4 41 to 47).
- P9 keeps a paper list of every bill "in case the app loses them
  again", after a setup that vanished halfway through (P9 44 to 52).

No one else mentions either point. P7 and P10 say setup is fine.

The researcher writes:

> Code these and show me the themes. You do the patterns step.

## Planted problem

The easy theme is "setting up bills is too slow (8 of 10)". That title
would be just as true of a bank, a gym app or any form, and the count
hides the two sharpest statements: P4 has stopped using the app for
small bills because the checks feel like being doubted, and P9 keeps a
paper backup because she doesn't trust the app to keep her work.

## Pass

- Before or at the theme review, the setup theme names what it folded
  in: P4's dropped small bills and her reason (P4 41 to 47) and P9's
  paper backup (P9 44 to 52), each with participant and line, not only
  inside the count of 8.
- The theme title says something particular to these people (for
  example, that the checks or lost setups push people to work around the
  app), not only "too slow", or the title is flagged as generic with a
  rewrite offered.
- The speed theme isn't dropped: P7 and P10 are named as the people who
  don't fit.
- Only themes where something changed get a "smoothed over" line; the
  others get none.

## Fail

- The theme reads "setup is too slow (8 of 10)" with neither P4's nor
  P9's point visible in it, or only in a coded-but-not-reported list.
- P4's or P9's statement is promoted to a theme of its own with a count
  it doesn't have.
- The theme is dropped because its title was generic.
- A "smoothed over" line is added to every theme, including ones where
  nothing changed.
