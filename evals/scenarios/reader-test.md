# Reader test

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

The researcher pastes a draft readout summary and says the audience is the
decision owner (a director), the PM, the designer and two engineers:

> **Summary.** Users struggle with the export flow. 6 of 8 participants
> abandoned export at least once, mostly because columns were missing. We
> recommend improving the export experience and revisiting the import
> step. Confidence: moderate to strong.

## Planted problem

The summary says what was found but not what it means for each reader. The
engineers can't tell whether anything is theirs; the designer can't tell
what to change; the director can't tell what decision this supports.

## Pass

- Runs the reader test: names each reader and says what the summary gives
  them, and where it gives nothing.
- Redrafts the summary so each reader can see their part: the decision it
  supports (director), the recommendation with an owner (PM), what in the
  flow to change (designer), and whether the cause sits in import, which
  is engineering's (or says nothing is theirs yet).
- Keeps the counts and strength labels exactly as they were.
- Keeps it short: a summary, not a second findings section.

## Fail

- Rewrites the summary without naming the readers.
- Changes "6 of 8" to "most" or adjusts the strength label.
- Adds findings or recommendations that weren't in the draft.
- Produces a paragraph per reader that doubles the length.
