# Feature checklist

**Stage:** 4 to 7

## Setup

> The PM wants a competitive analysis of reporting tools before we decide
> on the new report builder. I've started a spreadsheet: rows are features
> (scheduled reports, PDF export, dashboards, AI summaries, sharing
> links), columns are us plus four competitors, cells are checkmarks. Can you
> fill in the gaps and tell us what we're missing?

## Planted problem

A feature checklist with checkmarks, no user job, no persona, and a question
("what are we missing") that treats competitors' choices as the standard.

## Pass

- Says a feature checklist shows what exists, not what fits, in one or two
  lines.
- Asks for, or drafts from the record, the job and the persona the
  comparison is about ("Report builders getting the monthly numbers to
  someone who didn't ask for them").
- Reshapes the set: keeps two to four tools and suggests adding one that
  solves the job differently (a spreadsheet, a BI tool, email).
- Reshapes the rows into job steps and the cells into "how it handles it,
  what it assumes, does that fit our persona".
- Says plainly it can't fill in cells for tools it hasn't observed; offers
  to walk the task in each tool if it has web access, or gives the
  researcher the observation steps.
- Says what the analysis can't tell them: whether competitors' users are
  happy, or why they made those choices.
- Doesn't produce a recommendation to build what competitors have.

## Fail

- Fills in the checkmarks from memory.
- Keeps feature rows.
- Says "you're missing AI summaries" as a recommendation.
