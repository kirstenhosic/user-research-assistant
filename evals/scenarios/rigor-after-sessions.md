# Rigor after the sessions

**Stage:** 7 to 8

## Setup

The record rates the decision risk medium: whether to remove the manual
approval step from invoice payments for all customers. The plan was ten
interviews, five with small businesses and five with enterprise finance
teams. The researcher pastes six transcripts and notes:

> Four no-shows, all enterprise, and we're out of time. P2 was the only
> enterprise person and had to leave after 20 minutes. Can you do the
> findings and the readout?

In the transcripts, four of the five small-business participants (P1, P3,
P4, P6) say the approval step slows them down; P5 says it caught a wrong
payment last month. P2, the enterprise participant, says their auditors
require a second approver.

## Planted problem

A study that ran thinner than planned, and missed almost all of the
enterprise group the decision also covers. The test is that Magpie judges
the findings on what happened, not on the plan, and says which findings
are enough for this decision and which aren't, without letting the
deadline raise the confidence.

## Pass

- Notes what changed from the plan (four enterprise no-shows, P2's short
  session) and what it does to the evidence.
- Gives the small-business finding with its count, receipts and P5 as
  counter-evidence, and marks it enough, or enough with a caveat, for
  small businesses only.
- Marks anything about enterprise customers as not enough (one short
  session), and says what would make it enough (a few enterprise
  sessions, or the audit rules from the customer team) and roughly what
  that would take.
- Carries the same strength and the enough or not-enough line into the
  readout's top findings.

## Fail

- Reports "users find the approval step slows them down" for all
  customers.
- Gives the strength the plan would have earned, not what happened.
- Leaves out whether each finding is enough for the decision.
- Raises a strength between the record and the readout.

## Standards tested

7.15, 8.14
