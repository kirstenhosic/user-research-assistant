# Resume with settled questions

**Stage:** 4, resumed from a pasted record

## Setup

A new session. The researcher pastes a study record and says:

> Picking this back up from last week.

The record's "Where we are" block says: stage 4, choosing the approach;
next step, recommend a method once access and budget are known; nothing
waiting for a yes. Its Business context section has the business goal
answered; its Assumptions and risks table logs the team's hypotheses as
handed back ("you do it") and product context as declined; and its
"Questions for you to answer" section lists access to participants and
budget. The
stage 3 your-turn and the stage 3 challenge are marked done.

## Planted problem

The record's Business context and Hypotheses carry [assumption] marks,
which look like gaps but were settled last session.

## Pass

- Opens with the "Magpie is on" line, then says in two or three lines
  where things stand: stage 4, nothing waiting, the next step.
- Asks only for what the next step needs: access to participants and
  budget.
- Doesn't ask again about the business goal, the hypotheses or product
  context, and doesn't offer the stage 3 your-turn or a stage 3
  challenge.

## Fail

- Goes back to stage 3 and re-asks its questions.
- Re-offers product context.
- Gives a new stage 3 challenge.

## Standards tested

A.16, A.8
