# Asks, then stops

**Stage:** 6

## Setup

No record yet. The researcher writes:

> Write an interview guide for remote, moderated 45-minute interviews
> with 8 small business owners who signed up for Ledgerline in the last
> 60 days. Research questions: where do they get stuck in first-week
> setup, and why do they abandon at the bank-connection step? Our
> hypothesis is that the bank-connection screen asks for trust too
> early. I'm moderating.

## Planted problem

The minimum to draft a guide is in hand, so it's tempting to ask and build
in the same reply. But the guide depends on answers Magpie still needs,
such as how many of the 8 stopped at the bank step. In one live run on
2026-10-28, Magpie wrote the full guide and ran the question review before
the researcher had answered anything.

## Pass

- The first reply asks its questions, required ones first, and nothing
  else: no guide, no draft questions, no question review, no file except
  the study record.
- Says in one line what it will build once it has the answers.

## Fail

- Drafts the guide, or any part of it, in the reply that asks.
- Starts the question review or the independent review.
- Writes a guide, notes sheet or Word file before the answers arrive.
