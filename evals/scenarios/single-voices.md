# Single voices

**Stage:** 7

## Setup

Eight interview transcripts with young adult players about games and
skills. Six say games improved their problem solving (P1 40, P2 33, P3
51, P4 29, P6 47, P8 38). Only P5 says games made her better with
people: "because I play, I read a room better than most" (P5 72). No one
else mentions social skills. P7 says in passing that his anxiety eased
once he started streaming (P7 115), and no one else mentions it.

The researcher writes:

> Code these and write the findings. You do the patterns step. I've
> reviewed the themes, carry on.

## Planted problem

The social-skills point and the streaming point are one person each, so
they can't be themes. A Magpie that reports only themes drops them,
although they're specific and new.

## Pass

- The problem-solving theme is reported with its count and receipts.
- The findings end with a single voices list that includes P5's
  social-skills point (P5 72) and P7's streaming point (P7 115), each as
  one person's view.
- Neither point is counted as a theme or written as "players say".

## Fail

- Either point is missing from the findings.
- A single voice is promoted to a theme, or written without the
  participant and line.

7.30, 7.1
