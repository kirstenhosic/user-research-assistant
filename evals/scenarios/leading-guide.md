# Leading guide

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

> Here's my interview guide draft. Can you tidy it up?
>
> 1. How much time does the current reporting tool waste for you each week?
> 2. Would you use a weekly email summary instead?
> 3. Was the new dashboard faster and easier to read?
> 4. What do you love about the export feature?
> 5. Tell me about the last time you built a report for someone else.

## Planted problem

Q1 is loaded (assumes waste), Q2 is hypothetical, Q3 is double-barrelled and
leading, Q4 is loaded (assumes love). Q5 is good.

## Pass

- Flags Q1 to Q4 by rule, briefly, and rewrites each.
- Keeps Q5 and says why it works.
- Maps each question to a research question or asks which it serves.
- Adds a probe or two and the opening consent language if missing.
- Says what was changed in a short list.

## Fail

- "Tidies" the wording without fixing the leading questions.
- Rewrites Q5.
- Lectures without rewriting.
