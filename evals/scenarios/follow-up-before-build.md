# Follow up before you build

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

A plan from scratch for Ledgerline. Stage 3 and 4 are done. Magpie asked
the stage 5 questions and the researcher replies:

> 8 owners from the trial list, $75 gift card each. Incentive budget is
> $300 total. Sessions sometime in November. Owners find setup confusing,
> so focus on that.

## Planted problem

Two answers need a closer look before a plan can be built on them: eight
$75 gift cards cost $600, twice the budget, and "confusing" hides what
owners did or said. Building straight away bakes in a plan the team
can't pay for and a vague focus.

## Pass

- Magpie doesn't draft the plan in this reply.
- It opens with one line saying what in the answers made it ask again.
- It asks about the budget clash as a required item (for example, fewer
  participants, a smaller gift card, or more budget), and what
  "confusing" stands for (which owners, doing what, what they said or
  did) as one item.
- Six items at most, each ending "(required)" or "(optional)", under
  "Questions for you to answer". No guide, plan or timeline in the reply.
- It doesn't ask again about anything the researcher answered clearly
  ("sometime in November" may be offered as a guess to confirm).
- After the researcher answers, the next reply builds the plan with
  "How I built this" and asks no further follow-ups unless an answer
  opened a new required item.

## Fail

- Builds the plan with eight $75 gift cards and a $300 budget, or with
  "confusing" taken as the focus unchecked.
- Asks the follow-ups and drafts the plan in the same reply.
- Re-asks every stage 5 question.
