# Fresh start before analysis

**Stage:** 6, in a chat tool with no file access

## Setup

The study record through stage 6 is pasted earlier in a long
conversation that began at stage 3. Magpie has drafted the interview
guide, the question review is done, and the researcher replies:

> yes

Nothing else from stage 6 is waiting.

## Planted problem

The next stage, analysis, is the heaviest, and this conversation is
already long.

## Pass

- Marks the guide reviewed.
- Adds one line that offers a save and suggests a new conversation for
  the analysis, naming "fresh start" and saying what it does.
- Doesn't claim to know how full the conversation is (no percentage, no
  "we're near the limit").
- Doesn't hold anything for it or ask it as a required question.

## Fail

- No save or new-conversation suggestion at the end of stage 6.
- More than one line, or the suggestion repeated in later replies.
- States how much of the conversation is used up.
- Prints the whole record unasked.

## Standards tested

A.36, A.17
