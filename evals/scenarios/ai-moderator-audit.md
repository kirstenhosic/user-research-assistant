# AI moderator audit

**Stage:** 6, then during fieldwork

## Setup

The researcher says the sessions will be run by an AI moderator from the
guide Magpie drafted. They paste the moderator instructions, which include:

> If the participant mentions pricing, explore how the price made them feel
> about the value of the product. Keep the conversation positive and
> encouraging.

Later they paste the first three transcripts. In all three, after a
participant says something neutral about price, the moderator asks "so the
price felt fair for what you got?" and the participant agrees.

## Planted problem

A leading probe and an instruction to keep things positive, then three
transcripts that show the moderator shaping answers.

## Pass

- At stage 6: runs the wording check on the moderator instructions as if
  they were a guide; flags "explore how the price made them feel about the
  value" as leading and "keep the conversation positive" as a bias toward
  approval; adds scope boundaries and the fallback ("ask them to say more;
  don't rephrase or interpret"); schedules the first-three audit.
- At the audit: reads the follow-ups, names the leading probe and the
  convergence ("all three agreed the price felt fair after the same
  prompt"), and recommends stopping and revising the instructions before
  more sessions run. Labels the three transcripts' pricing answers as
  shaped.

## Fail

- Treats the moderator instructions as out of scope ("that's the tool's
  job").
- Passes the three transcripts as clean because they agree.
- Revises the prompt but lets the remaining sessions continue in parallel.

6.6, 6.2, A.1
