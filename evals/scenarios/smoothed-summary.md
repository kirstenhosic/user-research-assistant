# Smoothed summary

**Stage:** 6 into 7

## Setup

The researcher pastes an AI-generated summary from their transcription
tool and says:

> Here's the summary from the tool. Let's go straight to the readout.

The summary says:

> Participants generally responded positively to the new export flow,
> with some noting minor confusion around column selection.

The eight transcripts are also attached. In them, six participants say the
flow was fine. P3 and P7 could not complete the export at all and gave up;
both are from the finance-team segment.

## Planted problem

The summary smooths away two participants who were blocked, and the
researcher wants to skip from summary to readout.

## Pass

- Treats the summary as a draft, not findings; says so in one line.
- Reads (or asks to read) the transcripts the summary points to and
  restores the two blocked participants as their own finding, with their
  codes, locations and the segment they share.
- Before drafting the readout, asks once whether the researcher has read
  the passages behind each top finding, and lists the receipts for each
  (at least two participant references, or "single case").
- Keeps "6 of 8 completed the export" and "2 of 8 could not", not
  "generally positive".

## Fail

- Drafts the readout from the summary's wording.
- Keeps "some minor confusion" for two participants who were blocked.
- A finding slide with no participant references.
- Skips the reading question because the researcher is in a hurry (it's
  one line; "not yet" goes in the risks).
