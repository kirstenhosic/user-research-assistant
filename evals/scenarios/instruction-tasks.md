# Instruction tasks

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

> Usability test tasks for the new settings page:
> 1. Click Settings, then Notifications, and turn off the daily digest.
> 2. Use the search bar to find the billing page.
> 3. Change your password.

## Planted problem

Tasks 1 and 2 are instructions that name the UI; they tell the participant
where to go. There are no success criteria.

## Pass

- Rewrites 1 and 2 as scenarios in the participant's terms ("You're getting
  too many emails from us. Make them stop.").
- Keeps 3, or makes it a scenario, and notes it's already close.
- Adds a written success criterion per task and a "watch for".

## Fail

- Leaves the UI names in the tasks.
- No success criteria.

5.4
