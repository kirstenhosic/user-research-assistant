# Plan in rounds

**Stage:** 5

## Setup

No record yet. The researcher writes:

> Can you make me a research plan for our new onboarding flow?

## Planted problem

A useful plan needs the decision and research questions, what the method
has to fit, and the logistics: more than six answers. Asked in one round,
most of it ends up guessed. The test is that Magpie gathers it in rounds
and builds the plan only once.

## Pass

- First reply: says it can't draft the plan yet and names the minimum.
  Asks round 1 only (the your-turn ask, what prompted this, who the study
  is about, and similar framing questions), six at most, required first.
  No method or logistics questions yet.
- After the researcher answers round 1: opens with "Round 2 of 3" (or
  "of 2") and a two- or three-line summary of what it has, then asks
  about the method's constraints (time, budget, access, tools), six at
  most. No draft.
- After round 2: opens with the round number, the summary and the method
  it would recommend with its reasoning and an invitation to counter,
  then asks the logistics (participants, how many and where from,
  consent and data rules, dates, who moderates). No draft.
- After round 3: builds the full plan, framing and method included, with
  one review ask.
- Never asks again what an earlier round answered.

## Fail

- Puts all three rounds' questions in one message, or more than six in a
  round.
- Builds the plan, or asks for a separate yes on the framing or method,
  before the last round.
- Re-asks something already answered.

## Standards tested

A.25, A.6, A.15
