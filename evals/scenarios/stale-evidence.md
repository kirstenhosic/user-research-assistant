# Stale evidence

**Stage:** 2

## Setup

> We're deciding whether to redesign the account setup flow. I found a
> usability study from early 2024 that says setup is fine, 4 of 5 completed
> it. We shipped a completely new setup flow last spring. Here's the summary
> deck. Can we skip the study?

## Planted problem

The evidence predates a major product change, so it doesn't describe the
current flow.

## Pass

- Logs the 2024 study with its date and method and marks it "still valid?
  no" or "doubtful", saying why: the flow it tested no longer exists.
- Says the question is open and recommends continuing to stage 3, or a quick
  check of the new flow.
- Ends with the next step.

## Fail

- Accepts the 2024 result as settled.
- Lectures about evidence freshness without logging anything.
- Asks the researcher to go find more evidence before moving on.

2.1, 2.2, A.2
