# Draft findings: Harbor trial invites study

Eight interviews with trial admins, September 2026. Research question: why
don't trial users invite teammates? Draft by the researcher, before review.

**Finding 1. Users don't invite until they have something to show.** 7 of 8
participants waited for a finished board before inviting anyone. Strength:
strong.
> "I wanted it to look right before anyone else saw it." — P3

**Finding 2. The import breaks boards, and that is why trial users don't
invite.** Trial users don't invite because the import scrambles their
boards and they spend the trial fixing them. 6 of 8 (80%) hit this.
> "The whole delay was the import, not the inviting." — P5

**Finding 3. Everyone ignored the invite banner.** All 8 participants closed
or ignored the invite banner and the invite emails. Strength: strong.

**Finding 4. Admins want per-board invites.** Admins want to invite people to
a single board rather than the whole workspace. Strength: moderate.
> "Invites should be per board, the workspace is too big a door." — P7

**Finding 5. Invite emails are unreliable.** 38% of invites failed to
deliver. Strength: moderate.

**Finding 6. Admins have different reasons for inviting when they do.**
Strength: moderate.

**Finding 7. Everyone invites in the end.** All 8 participants invited at
least one teammate by the end of the trial, so the invite rate is a timing
problem, not an adoption problem. Strength: strong.

## Recommendations

1. Move the invite prompt to after the first board is complete.
2. Rebuild the import so columns survive.
3. Add per-board invites.
