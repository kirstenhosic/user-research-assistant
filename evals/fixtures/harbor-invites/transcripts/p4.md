# P4, 2026-09-09, trial admin, freelance designer

01 Interviewer: Can you tell me why you signed up for Harbor?
02 P4: I wanted somewhere to track my own client work. Just me.
03 Interviewer: Did you ever intend to invite anyone?
04 P4: No. I'm a one-person business. I don't have a team.
05 Interviewer: Did you share anything with clients?
06 P4: I thought about sharing a board with one client so they could see progress. I didn't in the end. I wasn't sure what they'd be able to see or change.
07 Interviewer: What stopped you?
08 P4: The share dialog has three options and I didn't understand the difference between "can view" and "can comment" for someone outside the workspace. I closed it.
09 Interviewer: Did you see any invite prompts?
10 P4: Constantly. The banner, two emails, a thing in the sidebar with "0 of 5 seats used". It's clearly built for teams.
11 Interviewer: How did that feel?
12 P4: Slightly like I was using it wrong. But the boards were good so I kept going.
13 Interviewer: What did you build during the trial?
14 P4: Four boards, one per client. Cards for each deliverable with due dates.
15 Interviewer: Did you convert to paid?
16 P4: Yes, the solo plan. Once I found there was one.
17 Interviewer: Anything that was hard?
18 P4: Finding the solo plan. The pricing page leads with teams.
19 Interviewer: Anything else?
20 P4: No, that's it. It works for me.
