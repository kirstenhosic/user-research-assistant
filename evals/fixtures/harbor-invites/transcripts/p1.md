# P1, 2026-09-08, trial admin, marketing agency (4 people)

01 Interviewer: Thanks for making time. Can you tell me about the first day you set up Harbor?
02 P1: Sure. I signed up on a Tuesday because our old board had just lost a week of cards. I made a workspace, named it after the agency, and started moving our client projects in.
03 Interviewer: What did you do first inside the workspace?
04 P1: Built the board for our biggest client. That took most of the afternoon because I was copying cards by hand.
05 Interviewer: Did you invite anyone that day?
06 P1: No. I wanted it to look right before anyone else saw it. If I invite people into a half-empty board they'll judge the tool by that.
07 Interviewer: When did you invite someone?
08 P1: Day three, I think. Once the client board was done I shared it with our account manager and then invited her properly so she could edit.
09 Interviewer: What made day three the moment?
10 P1: The board was finished. There was something to show. I invited her so she could comment on it, honestly, not so she could build anything.
11 Interviewer: Was there anything in the product prompting you to invite people before that?
12 P1: There was a banner on day one. "Invite your team." I closed it. It felt like a sales thing.
13 Interviewer: Did anything stop you from inviting earlier, apart from wanting the board done?
14 P1: Not really. The invite thing itself was easy. I pasted two emails and it went.
15 Interviewer: How did the account manager react?
16 P1: She liked it. She added a column the same day. That was the point where I thought, ok, this is going to stick.
17 Interviewer: Did you invite anyone else during the trial?
18 P1: The other two, a week later. Same reason: once there was something for them to look at.
19 Interviewer: What would have made you invite everyone on day one?
20 P1: If there had been a way to bring our old board in automatically. Then it would have been full on day one and I'd have had no reason to wait.
21 Interviewer: Anything else about the trial that stood out?
22 P1: The trial countdown was stressful. Fourteen days and I'd used three just copying cards.
