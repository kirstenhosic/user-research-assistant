# P7, 2026-09-11, trial admin, research coordinator at a university lab

01 Interviewer: Tell me about setting up Harbor.
02 P7: I set it up for a grant project. Six people across two institutions.
03 Interviewer: What did you do first?
04 P7: I made a board per work package. Four boards. That took two evenings because I was writing the task descriptions as I went.
05 Interviewer: Did you invite anyone in that time?
06 P7: No. Our PI would have wanted to see something complete. I waited until all four boards were filled in.
07 Interviewer: And then?
08 P7: Then I invited everyone at once. Day six.
09 Interviewer: Did you share a board link with anyone before that?
10 P7: I sent the PI a shared link to one board on day four to check he was happy with the structure. He replied "looks good" and that was it. Then I finished the other three and invited the group.
11 Interviewer: How did the group take it?
12 P7: Two of them at the other institution couldn't get in. Their university email filtered the invite. I had to send a link by hand.
13 Interviewer: Did Harbor help with that?
14 P7: Not really. The invite page said "pending" and nothing else. I found out from them directly.
15 Interviewer: Was there anything that prompted you to invite earlier?
16 P7: The banner and an email. I knew they were there. I wasn't ready.
17 Interviewer: What would have made you ready sooner?
18 P7: If I could have invited people to one board rather than the whole workspace. I'd have brought the PI in on day two for the first board.
19 Interviewer: Is that possible in Harbor?
20 P7: I don't think so. I looked. Invites are workspace-level.
21 Interviewer: Anything else?
22 P7: The trial ran out before the second institution was properly in. We paid anyway.
