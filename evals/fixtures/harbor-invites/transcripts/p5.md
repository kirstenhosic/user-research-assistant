# P5, 2026-09-10, trial admin, engineering manager, 12-person team

01 Interviewer: How did the trial start for you?
02 P5: Badly, to be honest. I signed up, imported our issues from the old tracker, and the import put every issue on one board with no columns.
03 Interviewer: What did you do?
04 P5: Spent a day sorting it out. Built columns, dragged things around. Then it looked right.
05 Interviewer: Did you invite anyone during that day?
06 P5: No way. If my team had seen that board they would have laughed and gone back to the old tool.
07 Interviewer: When did you invite people?
08 P5: Day two, after it was tidy. I shared the main board link in our chat first and asked for reactions. Got a couple of thumbs up. Then I invited the whole team, all twelve.
09 Interviewer: So the share came first and the invites followed.
10 P5: Yes, same day though. The share was a sanity check.
11 Interviewer: How did the invite itself go?
12 P5: Fine. I pasted twelve emails. Eleven worked. One bounced because of a typo on my side.
13 Interviewer: What was the team's reaction?
14 P5: Mixed. Half of them just kept using the old tracker for a week. Two never logged in at all during the trial.
15 Interviewer: Did the product do anything to help with that?
16 P5: I got an email saying three teammates hadn't accepted. That was useful, actually. I pinged them.
17 Interviewer: What would have made day one better?
18 P5: An import that kept my columns. The whole delay was the import, not the inviting.
19 Interviewer: Anything else?
20 P5: The seat counter in the sidebar was wrong for a day. It said 5 of 5 when we had 12 on. It sorted itself out.
