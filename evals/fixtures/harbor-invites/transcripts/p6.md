# P6, 2026-09-10, trial admin, office manager at a dental practice

01 Interviewer: What were you hoping Harbor would do for you?
02 P6: Track the things that have to happen every week at the practice. Orders, cleaning, equipment checks. We had it on a whiteboard.
03 Interviewer: What did you do on your first day?
04 P6: I made a board that copied the whiteboard. Took about twenty minutes. Then I showed it to the practice owner on my laptop.
05 Interviewer: Did you share it or invite him at that point?
06 P6: Neither. I just turned the laptop round. He said "fine, if it's not expensive."
07 Interviewer: When did you invite anyone?
08 P6: Day one, actually, right after that. I invited the two nurses because they're the ones who do the checks. I needed them in it or the board was just me again.
09 Interviewer: So no shared link first.
10 P6: No. I didn't know there was a share link until you just said it. I only knew about inviting.
11 Interviewer: How was inviting?
12 P6: Easy enough. One of them didn't get the email, so I sent it again and it worked.
13 Interviewer: Did they use it?
14 P6: One did straight away. The other one I had to show in person. She's not a computer person.
15 Interviewer: Was there anything in the product that prompted you to invite?
16 P6: The big banner. I'd have done it anyway, but the banner is where I found the button.
17 Interviewer: What would have stopped you inviting on day one?
18 P6: If it had been complicated. It wasn't. The board was done in twenty minutes and there was nothing to wait for.
19 Interviewer: Anything else?
20 P6: We've stayed on it. The whiteboard is gone.
