# P3, 2026-09-09, trial admin, operations lead at a logistics firm

01 Interviewer: Tell me about your first week with Harbor.
02 P3: First week was me alone. I'm not technical, so I set up one board for dispatch and played with it until I understood it.
03 Interviewer: What did you do on day one?
04 P3: Made the dispatch board and a card for every truck. Took an hour.
05 Interviewer: Did you invite anyone on day one?
06 P3: No. I didn't know if I'd keep it.
07 Interviewer: When did you invite someone?
08 P3: Day four or five. I shared the dispatch board with my deputy as a link first, to see what she thought. She said it was clearer than the spreadsheet, so I invited her as a member the same afternoon.
09 Interviewer: So the share came before the invite.
10 P3: Yes. Sharing is low commitment. Inviting someone is asking them to learn a tool.
11 Interviewer: What happened after you invited her?
12 P3: She moved a few cards and found a mistake I'd made. Which was useful. Then I invited the two dispatchers.
13 Interviewer: Did the product prompt you to invite people?
14 P3: There was a thing at the top. I don't remember what it said. I ignored most of the banners.
15 Interviewer: Was inviting easy when you did it?
16 P3: I had to look for it. I expected it under the board and it was in the workspace settings. Found it in a minute or two.
17 Interviewer: What would have made you invite people on day one?
18 P3: Nothing. I wasn't going to invite people to something I didn't understand yet.
19 Interviewer: Anything else from the trial that stuck with you?
20 P3: I liked that nothing nagged me by email every day. One or two is fine.
