# P8, 2026-09-11, trial admin, founder of a two-person startup

01 Interviewer: How did you start with Harbor?
02 P8: My cofounder and I both signed up separately on the same day without telling each other. Classic.
03 Interviewer: What happened?
04 P8: We ended up with two workspaces. I deleted mine and he invited me to his. So I was invited, not inviting.
05 Interviewer: In his workspace, had he built anything first?
06 P8: One board, half done. He invited me within an hour of signing up, I think. We were in a call anyway.
07 Interviewer: So no waiting until something was finished.
08 P8: No. It's the two of us. There's no one to impress.
09 Interviewer: Did either of you share a board link before inviting?
10 P8: No. Why would we? We just invited.
11 Interviewer: Did you see the invite prompts?
12 P8: I saw the banner when I made my own workspace. I'd have used it if he hadn't got there first.
13 Interviewer: What did you two build in the trial?
14 P8: A roadmap board and a bugs board. We still use both.
15 Interviewer: Anything hard?
16 P8: Merging the two workspaces. There's no way to do it. Deleting mine was the only option.
17 Interviewer: Did anything else slow you down?
18 P8: No. We were live in an hour.
19 Interviewer: Anything else?
20 P8: It would be nice if Harbor noticed two people from the same domain signing up on the same day and offered to join them up.
