# Draft readout summary: Tally receipt capture usability test

Six moderated sessions, 1 to 3 October 2026. Two tasks: capture and submit a
paper receipt; find last month's expenses and check approvals. Draft by the
researcher, before review.

**Summary.** Receipt capture is generally positive with some minor confusion.
83% of participants completed the capture task, a statistically significant
majority. Everyone completed the approvals task. Confidence: strong.

**Finding 1. Capture works.** 5 of 6 (83%) completed task 1, and 4 of 6
completed it in under a minute. Strength: strong.
> "That's quicker than our current one." — P3

**Finding 2. The scanner misreads receipts.** Most participants had the
scanner misread the amount or the date. Strength: strong.
> "It's read the four as a one, I'll have to check every time." — P2

**Finding 3. Participants can't tell whether an expense was submitted.**
Participants were confused about whether their expense had been submitted.
Strength: moderate.

**Finding 4. Users want offline capture.** Field staff need capture to work
without a signal. Strength: moderate.
> "If it doesn't, I'll be taking photos in the camera app and doing this at the hotel, which is what I do now." — P3

**Finding 5. The approvals list is clear.** All six read the tick and clock
icons correctly and found their approvals without help. Strength: strong.
