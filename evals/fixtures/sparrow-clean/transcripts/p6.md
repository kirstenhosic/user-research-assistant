# P6, 2026-10-10, hotel restaurant manager, 25 staff

01 Interviewer: How do swaps happen on your team?
02 P6: Same as everywhere, I imagine.
03 Interviewer: Tell me how it works for you.
04 P6: Group chat. They post the swap, I reply OK, and it's agreed.
05 Interviewer: How does the rota keep up?
06 P6: I print the week's rota and pin it in the office.
07 Interviewer: And when a swap happens midweek?
08 P6: I cross it out on the printout and write the new name in. Then I update Sparrow at the end of the week. I always know who's on, because the printout is the real rota.
09 Interviewer: Anything you'd change?
10 P6: I'd want swaps to need my OK before they count. Right now OK in the chat is my OK, which works because I'm always in the chat.
