# P2, 2026-10-06, café manager, 9 staff

01 Interviewer: How do swaps work at your café?
02 P2: People sort it between themselves, mostly.
03 Interviewer: Where does that happen?
04 P2: On our group chat. Someone asks, someone answers, done.
05 Interviewer: How do you find out?
06 P2: If I'm reading the chat, I see it. I'm not always reading the chat.
07 Interviewer: What happens when you miss one?
08 P2: I lose track. The rota says one thing and the chat says another, and the chat wins because that's what people read.
09 Interviewer: Has that caused a problem?
10 P2: Once, badly. A swap never made it onto the rota, the person who'd swapped out assumed they were off, the other one forgot, and nobody opened on a Tuesday.
11 Interviewer: What did you do after that?
12 P2: Asked everyone to tell me directly. That lasted about two weeks.
