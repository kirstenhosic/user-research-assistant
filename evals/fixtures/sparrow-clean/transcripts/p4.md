# P4, 2026-10-08, kitchen manager, 30 staff

01 Interviewer: How are shift swaps arranged in your kitchen?
02 P4: It depends who's asking, but mostly the same way.
03 Interviewer: Which way is that?
04 P4: The staff group chat. It's quickest because everyone has it open.
05 Interviewer: How does the rota keep up?
06 P4: Badly. I mean to update it the same day.
07 Interviewer: And when that doesn't happen?
08 P4: I lose track of who swapped with who, especially across two sites. I've had two people turn up for one shift.
09 Interviewer: Do swaps cost you anything?
10 P4: Not really. Overtime is usually sickness, not swaps. Swaps are a muddle, not a cost.
