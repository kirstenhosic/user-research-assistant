# Draft findings: Larder meal-planning diary study

Six participants kept a diary for two weeks, 5 to October 18 2026, with an
exit interview each. Research question: how does weekly meal planning fit
into people's weeks, and what makes them stop? Draft by the researcher,
before review.

**Finding 1. Planning is a daily habit.** Users plan meals in Larder about
four times a week, mostly in the evening around 6pm. Strength: strong.

**Finding 2. Everyone got faster at planning by week two.** 4 of 6 (75%)
took less time on their second weekly plan, because they learned the app.
Strength: strong.
> "By week two it kept suggesting the same five recipes, so I was searching more." — P4

**Finding 3. Planning drops off at weekends.** P3 abandons planning at
weekends. Strength: moderate.
> "Skipped planning this weekend." — P3

**Finding 4. The shopping list is underused.** Only P2 and P6 used the
shopping list. P5 never used it. Strength: moderate.

**Finding 5. Recipe suggestions delight people.** Strength: moderate.
> "Larder suggested chickpea curry and I added it." — P6

**Finding 6. Price per meal is what keeps people planning.** Strength:
moderate.

## Recommendations

1. Add a daily 6pm reminder to Larder, since that's when people plan.
2. Promote the shopping list in onboarding, since few people find it.
3. Add price per meal.
